# HoverSling (Split-Leg & Repositioning Sheet) — Instructions for Use (Manufacturer IFU)

Source: HoverTech International official User Manual, "HSManual, Rev. J" (dated Nov 2024). Company internal copy from SharePoint `Manuals 2024`.
Curated: 2026-07-09.

## Intended use
Combination air-assisted transfer mattress and lift sling. As an air-assisted mattress, the Air Supply inflates it to cushion the patient while air escapes underneath, reducing transfer force by 80-90% — used for lateral transfers, positioning (boosting, turning), proning. Used uninflated (as a sling) with a loop-style hanger bar for vertical lifts, turning, and proning.

## Indications
Patients unable to assist in their own lateral or vertical transfer; patients whose weight/girth poses a risk to caregivers.

## Contraindications
Unstable thoracic, cervical, or lumbar fractures — do not use unless a clinical decision has been made by the facility.

## Care settings
Hospitals, long-term or extended care facilities.

## Precautions — as a transfer mattress
- Verify brakes engaged before transfer; minimum two caregivers for lateral transfers.
- Never leave patient unattended on an inflated HoverSling; never move a patient laterally on an uninflated one.
- Ensure patient is centered before inflating.
- Low-air-loss bed transfers: set bed airflow to highest level for a firm surface.
- OR use: always deflate and secure the patient/HoverSling to the OR table before angling the table.

## Precautions — as a sling
- Only trained personnel should use it as a sling. A risk assessment (correct size) and a clinical assessment by a qualified nurse/therapist are required before lifting a patient with it.
- Do not use the Split-Leg lateral transfer handles to lift — excessive force can tear the sling.
- Match shoulder/leg strap loop colors left-to-right when attaching to the hanger bar; confirm straps are properly connected before lifting.
- Never lift higher than necessary; never leave the patient unattended.
- If weight limits differ between the lift, hanger bar, and HoverSling, **the lowest limit applies**.
- Any indication of damage: remove from service and discard.

## Step-by-step: Split-Leg as a transfer mattress
1. Ensure sling support straps are stowed in strap pockets and the 4 snaps at the center leg/foot sections are connected.
2. Patient supine. Place underneath using log-roll; secure patient strap loosely.
3. Plug in Air Supply, insert hose nozzle into one of two hose entries at the foot end, snap into place.
4. Lock all wheels, surfaces as close as possible; transfer high-to-low surface if possible.
5. Turn on Air Supply; push at an angle (head or feet first); opposite caregiver grasps handles halfway across and pulls to position.
6. Center patient on receiving equipment before deflation; turn off, raise rails, unbuckle strap.

## Step-by-step: Split-Leg as a sling — patient in bed
1. Log-roll (or lean-and-slide) the sling under the patient, horseshoe apex at the coccyx. Elevate head of bed.
2. Attach shoulder straps to the hanger bar (color-coded loops; shorter blue/beige = more upright, longer white = more recline/less hip flexion).
3. Release inside leg snaps, cross inside leg straps through each other, attach to hanger bar.
4. Attach outside leg support straps to hanger bar.
Patient in chair: same strap logic, lean patient forward, position horseshoe apex behind patient centered on torso, leg sections placed under each leg.
Back to bed: position patient above bed center, raise head of bed, lower onto bed; detach strap loops; restow straps in pockets.

## Step-by-step: Repositioning Sheet as a transfer mattress
Same core sequence as Split-Leg transfer mattress use (log-roll, hose entry at foot end, lock wheels, inflate, push-and-pull via handles, center, deflate, unbuckle). Note: for the 50" size, two air supplies may be used for inflation.

## Step-by-step: Repositioning Sheet as a sling
- **Patient in bed:** place under patient via log-roll; attach all 8 sling support straps to the hanger bar (color-coded); lift per the lift manufacturer's instructions.
- **Lateral turn/prone:** center patient, lower hanger bar to working height, raise bed rails, attach all 4 straps on the side opposite the turn direction (starting at foot end) to the same-side hanger-bar loops; as the lift raises, the patient turns toward the opposite side. Use wedges to hold position if desired.
- **Hygiene turn:** center patient, lower hanger bar, raise rails, attach only the strap closest to the patient's shoulder; as the lift raises, the patient turns to the side opposite that strap.
- **Seated transfer (from bed or chair):** attach head straps to the hanger bar (transfer handle for fully upright, first sling strap for a reclined/reduced-hip-flexion position), attach the blue shoulder strap each side, fold the sling between the patient's legs, cross one foot-end strap through the other and attach to the hanger bar, then transfer.
- **Attaching Link straps (bedframe anchoring):** thread through the bedframe/headboard/siderail per facility protocol, fasten Velcro hook to loop on each side, then footend. Remove Link straps before moving the patient; store in side pockets when not in use.

## Specifications
- **HoverSling Split-Leg**: top polyester knit, bottom nylon twill, sewn. 34" (86cm) x 70" (178cm). Model HMSLING-34-B. **Weight limit 700 lbs / 318 kg.**
- **HoverSling Repositioning Sheet**: same materials, 39" (99cm) or 50" (127cm) x 78" (198cm). Models HMSLING-39RS-B / -50RS-B (and -TVA variants). **Weight limit 1000 lbs / 454 kg.**
- Required air-supply accessory (transfer-mattress mode): same HTAIR1200/2300/1000/2356 or AIR200G/400G family as HoverMatt.
- Required accessory (sling mode): any patient lift with 2/3/4-point loop-style hanger bar (mobile hoist, hoist trolley, stationary hoist).

## Cleaning and maintenance
Single-patient use — **not intended to be laundered**; discard if grossly soiled. Use HoverCover or disposable sheets on top to help keep clean. Check before each use: all straps/handles intact, "Do Not Launder" label intact, no tears/holes/loose threads — discard if damaged. **Replace if the patient's stay exceeds three months**, even without visible damage.

## Regulatory note
Conforms to Medical Device Regulation (EU) 2017/745, Class 1, and additionally **ISO 10535** (the hoist/lifting-equipment standard) given its dual use as a sling.
