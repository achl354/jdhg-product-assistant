# HoverMatt Air Transfer System — Instructions for Use (Manufacturer IFU)

Source: HoverTech International official User Manual, "HMManual, Rev. M" (dated 2023-10-04). Company internal copy from SharePoint `Manuals 2024`; public manufacturer site fetch was blocked, so this is the manufacturer's own document text, not a paraphrase from a secondary source.
Curated: 2026-07-09.

## Intended use
Assists caregivers with patient transfers, positioning (boosting, turning), and proning. The HoverTech Air Supply inflates the HoverMatt to cushion/cradle the patient while air escapes from underside holes, reducing the force needed to move the patient by 80-90%.

## Indications
- Patients unable to assist in their own lateral transfer.
- Patients whose weight/girth poses a potential health risk to caregivers repositioning or transferring them.

## Contraindications
- Patients experiencing unstable thoracic, cervical, or lumbar fractures should not use the HoverMatt unless a clinical decision has been made by the facility.

## Intended care settings
Hospitals, long-term or extended care facilities.

## Precautions — Air Supply
- Not for use in the presence of flammable anesthetics, or in a hyperbaric chamber or oxygen tent.
- Route the power cord to avoid hazard; avoid blocking air intakes.
- MRI use requires a 25 ft specialty MRI hose (purchased separately).
- Avoid electric shock — do not open the air supply unit.

## Precautions — HoverMatt
- Caregivers must verify all brakes are engaged before transfer.
- **Minimum two caregivers** for air-assisted lateral patient transfers (in-bed positioning tasks may need more than one).
- Never leave the patient unattended on an inflated device.
- Only use HoverTech-authorized attachments/accessories.
- When transferring to/from a low-air-loss bed, set the bed mattress airflow to the highest level for a firm transfer surface.
- **Never attempt to move a patient on an uninflated HoverMatt.**
- Side rails must be raised by one caregiver.
- In the OR: always deflate the HoverMatt and secure the patient/HoverMatt to the OR table before angling the table, to prevent slipping.

## Step-by-step: HoverMatt / HoverMatt SPU lateral transfer
1. Patient should preferably be supine.
2. Place the HoverMatt underneath the patient using a log-rolling technique; secure patient straps loosely.
3. Plug the HoverTech Air Supply power cord into an electrical outlet.
4. Insert the hose nozzle into either of the two intake valves at the foot end — snap into place and close the flap.
5. Ensure transfer surfaces are as close as possible and lock all wheels.
6. If possible, transfer from a higher surface to a lower surface.
7. Turn on the HoverTech Air Supply.
8. Push the HoverMatt at an angle, headfirst or feetfirst. Halfway across, the opposite caregiver grasps the closest handles and pulls to the desired location.
9. Ensure the patient is centered on the receiving equipment before deflation.
10. Turn off the air supply, raise the bed/stretcher rails, unbuckle patient straps.

Transfer handles: 8 on Single-Patient Use (SPU) models, 6 on reusable models. Do not pull the patient straps or use them as transfer handles.

## Step-by-step: HoverMatt SPU Link (adds bedframe anchoring)
- **Attaching to bedframe:** remove Link straps from pockets, loosely attach to solid bed-frame points so the mat can move with the patient. Before transfers/positioning, disconnect and stow the Link straps.
- **Lateral transfer:** same 10-step sequence as standard HoverMatt above, then after step 10, re-attach the Link straps to the bed frame.
- Note: for patients of size, or when more lift is needed, two air supplies may be used for inflation.

## Step-by-step: HoverMatt SPU Split-Leg (lithotomy position)
1. Separate legs into two sections by disconnecting the snaps.
2. Position each section on the table with the patient's legs.
3. For lateral transfer: reconnect all snaps at the center leg/foot sections first, then follow the same log-roll → intake valve → lock wheels → inflate → push-and-pull → center → deflate → unbuckle sequence as the standard HoverMatt.
4. Once deflated, position each leg section as appropriate.

## Step-by-step: HoverMatt Half-Matt / SPU Half-Matt
Same core sequence as the standard HoverMatt (log-roll, intake valves, lock wheels, inflate, push-and-pull, center, deflate, unbuckle).
**Precaution: use a minimum of THREE caregivers** for air-assisted lateral transfers with the Half-Matt and SPU Half-Matt (higher than the standard two-caregiver minimum) — footend caregiver guides the patient's feet during the transfer.

## Using the HoverMatt in the Operating Room
Can be used to transfer/position/reposition patients to and from the OR. A patient may ambulate onto a pre-positioned HoverMatt on the OR table, or it can be deployed in the typical fashion (e.g. stretcher/bed to OR table transfer).
1. Follow the standard lateral-transfer steps above.
2. Ensure the OR table is locked before initiating a lateral transfer.
3. Ensure the HoverMatt's edges are tucked under the OR table mattress after transfer.

**HoverMatt T-Burg**: intended for surgical procedures placing the patient in Trendelenburg (or Reverse Trendelenburg) up to 40 degrees, including with robotics. 400 lb weight limit. See the separate T-Burg manual for full instructions.

## HT-Air Air Supply — keypad
- **ADJUSTABLE** button: 4 pressure/inflation-rate settings (each press increases pressure/rate; green flashing LED count indicates the setting). Settings are all lower than HoverMatt/HoverJack transfer settings — **not to be used for transferring**; it's a comfort/centering feature for timid or pained patients.
- **STANDBY**: stops inflation/airflow (amber LED).
- Two power-head variants: one for 28"/34" HoverMatts and HoverSlings, one for 39"/50" HoverMatts/HoverSlings and 32"/39" HoverJacks.
- **Warning:** the HT-Air is not compatible with DC power supplies and is not for use with the HoverJack Battery Cart.

## Specifications
**Reusable HoverMatt** — Heat-Sealed: nylon twill. Double-Coated: nylon twill with polyurethane coating on the patient side. RF-welded construction.
- Widths: 28" (71cm) HM28HS/DC, 34" (86cm) HM34HS/DC, 39" (99cm) HM39HS/DC, 50" (127cm) HM50HS/DC — length 78" (198cm). **Weight limit 1200 lbs / 544 kg.**
- Half-Matt: 34" x 45" (114cm), HM-Mini34HS/DC. **Weight limit 600 lbs / 272 kg.**

**HoverMatt SPU** — top: non-woven polypropylene fiber; bottom: nylon twill; sewn construction.
- Widths 34"/39"/50", weight limit 1200 lbs/544 kg (Half-Matt variant: 600 lbs/272 kg).
- Model families: HM_SPU-B (standard), HMHC (with HoverCover), HM34SPU-SPLIT-B (split-leg), HM_SPU-LNK-B (Link).

**Required air supply accessory** (same for all HoverMatt variants): HTAIR1200 (North America, 120V 60Hz 10A), HTAIR2300 (Europe, 230V 50Hz 6A), HTAIR1000 (Japan), HTAIR2356 (Korea), or AIR200G/AIR400G canister units.

## Cleaning and maintenance (reusable only)
- Between patients: wipe down with hospital-approved disinfection solution, a 10:1 bleach solution, or disinfecting wipes. Follow the cleaning product's dwell-time/saturation instructions. Bleach may discolor fabric.
- If badly soiled: machine launder, **max water temperature 160°F (71°C)**; a 10:1 bleach solution may be used during the wash.
- Air dry if possible (can circulate air through the mattress using the air supply to speed drying). If using a dryer: coolest setting, **never exceed 115°F (46°C)** — the polyurethane backing deteriorates with repeated high-temperature drying.
- **The Double-Coated HoverMatt must never go in the dryer.**
- The SPU HoverMatt is not intended to be laundered at all.
- HoverCover disposable absorbent cover, or disposable sheets, can be used on top to help keep the HoverMatt clean.

## Preventive maintenance (pre-use check)
Visual inspection before use: no visible damage, all patient straps/handles present, no tears or holes that would prevent inflation. If damage would prevent normal function: remove from use and return to HoverTech for repair (SPU HoverMatts: discard instead).

## Infection control
Heat-sealed construction eliminates the needle holes of a sewn mattress (a potential bacterial entry point). Double-Coated adds a stain/fluid-proof surface. SPU HoverMatt eliminates cross-contamination risk and the need for laundering entirely. For isolation patients, apply the same protocol the facility uses for bed mattresses/linens in that room.
