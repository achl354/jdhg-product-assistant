## Intended use
A patient positioning system for surgical procedures using the Trendelenburg position — keeps the patient static and fixed in place to prevent sliding on the surgical table while tilted up to 40°. Used in gynecology, urology, and conventional/robotic-assisted laparoscopic procedures.

## Warnings — personal injury hazard
- Read and understand all instructions before attaching/removing TrenGuard.
- Theatre staff must complete appropriate training before positioning a patient with TrenGuard.
- **Do not use** if TrenGuard components, rail clamps, or OR table side rails are damaged.
- **Never attach the Patient Support Frame to the removable headrest/head section of an OR table** — mount only to the torso/back section's accessory rails.
- Always inspect all components before use; do not use if Velcro on the frame or pillows is worn, peeling, or damaged, or if any component shows visible damage/wear.
- **Do not use with patients weighing more than 250 kg.**
- **Do not use in reverse Trendelenburg.**
- Do not use with the table articulated in lateral tilt without first deploying additional positioning equipment to prevent sideways sliding.
- Use compatible wide-based slotted rail clamps only — **never narrow-based slotted rail clamps.**

## Warnings — infection hazard
- Do not reuse single-use components.
- Reusable components must be cleaned and dried before use.
- Do not submerge; do not heat sterilize.

## Product components (all required for use)
1. TrenGuard Patient Support Frame
2. Cervical Notch Bolster ("Speed Bump") — single-use
3. Head Stabilizer — single-use
4. Lateral Stabilizing Pillows (Hybrid Reusable version, or single-use, depending on pack)

Also required: compatible rail clamps for the specific OR table's accessory rail type.

**Not appropriate for:** patients with severe kyphosis, obstructions preventing direct neck contact, no discernible cervical notch, or other conditions preventing them lying flat on their back with the Speed Bump in the cervical notch.

## Step-by-step: setup and installation
**Step 1 — pre-checks:** choose the correct pack for the patient's weight/anatomy (see table above). Open the pack at least 10 minutes before use and inspect all components (packs may lose their vacuum seal and partially inflate — still usable; if components aren't fully inflated after 10 minutes, open a new pack). Confirm rail clamps are defect-free. Confirm the OR table is properly located with floor locks engaged and the head section removed. Mount the correct **wide-based slotted rail clamps** to the torso/back section's accessory rails (never the head section).

**Step 2 — install the Patient Support Frame:** insert the frame's legs/posts into the rail clamps, centred across the table width, with the extension facing the foot end. Leave clamps loose for now (adjust later). Cover the Velcro with a lint-free towel to protect the patient's skin during transfer. Position the anaesthesia "tree" off to one side under the mattress at the head end. Always place the frame on top of any under-patient devices (warming pads, grounding pads, gel pads, air-assisted transfer devices like the HoverMatt).

**Step 3 — transfer the patient** onto the OR table in the usual manner and position for the procedure.

**Step 4 — attach the "Speed Bump" (cervical notch bolster):** remove any protective covering from the frame's Velcro. Place the bolster into the frame's central recess, short extension pointing toward the foot end. Ensure the hump section extends beyond the frame by at least a finger's width (≥1.3cm) on the side closest to the foot end. Press firmly to secure. For high-BMI patients with no discernible cervical concavity, substitute an Integrated Support Wedge (two sizes available) — thin end toward the foot end, hump extending ~25mm beyond the frame's leading edge.

**Step 5 — final position before draping:** lift the patient's head, slide the frame toward the shoulders until the Speed Bump's hump section rests against the trapezius muscles in direct skin contact, then lower the head. Visually confirm the hump is nesting into the cervical notch. Repeat if not correctly positioned. Once satisfied, **securely tighten both slotted rail clamps** to anchor the frame to the table's accessory rails. (The Speed Bump/Integrated Wedge is the *only* restraint mechanism in the system — correct nesting against the trapezius is what prevents sliding, even in steep Trendelenburg.)

**Step 6 — install the Head Stabilizing Pillow:** lift the patient's head, slide the pillow under the occiput so its slotted underside straddles the Speed Bump's long extension, lower the head into the foam concavity. **Check the patient's ears are not compressed by the pillow's leading edge.**

**Step 7 — install the Lateral Stabilizing Pillows (both sides):** lift one shoulder, tilt the pillow to avoid Velcro contact while placing it against the shoulder at an angle to the frame (initial gap ~5cm/hand-width from the shoulder), then lower the shoulder to secure it to the Velcro. Repeat on the other side. These are non-structural — they control body-mass shift during the transition to extreme Trendelenburg, and because they are *not* shoulder bolsters, patients are not at risk of brachial plexus neuropathy from this restraint method.

**Step 8 — confirm installation:** re-confirm both rail clamps are tightened and both pillows are secured to the frame. **Do not tilt the table until this is confirmed.**

**Step 9 — tilt test (always before draping):** tilt the OR table to the maximum Trendelenburg degree anticipated for the procedure, hold for at least 5 seconds, then return to horizontal. **Repeat steps 5–9 whenever the patient needs to be repositioned**, and re-confirm rail clamps are tightened before each repositioning. Follow hospital protocols on tilt speed, sequence, and duration.

## Cleaning and storage
Follow the hospital's cleaning/hygiene/disinfection protocol for table accessories and Velcro — hospital-grade disinfectant wipes (e.g. Quaternary Ammonium Compound "Quats" wipes) to dampen and wipe down reusable components, with particular attention to areas where fluid migration could occur. **Do not submerge. Do not heat sterilize.** After disinfecting and drying, store the Support Frame and Hybrid Reusable Lateral Stabilizing Pillows in a secure location at room temperature, away from extreme temperatures.

## Replacement parts, service life, and warranty
- JD Healthcare Group warrants products free from manufacturing defects for **12 months** from delivery to the end user (per the 2024 JDHG guide — note the original manufacturer's own document states 6 months; follow JDHG's 12-month figure for JDHG-supplied product).
- **Patient Support Frame useful life: 3 years**, with periodic Velcro maintenance.
- **Hybrid Lateral Stabilizing Pillows useful life: 1 year or 500 uses**, whichever comes first (marketing material separately states they can be washed/reused up to 150 times — treat the IFU's "1 year or 500 uses" as the governing limit).
- All other components are single-use only.
- Always check Velcro integrity before use — replace if peeling, creeping, or worn.
- If single-use foam pillows don't fully inflate after 10 minutes, open a new pack and contact your local supplier for a replacement.
- Report any serious incident involving this device to JD Healthcare Group and the relevant state authority.

## Context (marketing material — not a regulatory claim)
JDHG's product summary describes TrenGuard as used in over 500,000 procedures (US and Australia combined) with no reported incidents of patient sliding, post-operative pain, or positioning-related nerve injury; installation is described as completable in under 20 seconds with the tilt test adding under 4.5 minutes, and reusable components as cleanable in under 90 seconds. Also noted: TrenGuard is designed to work in conjunction with the **HoverMatt Split-Leg Matt** for lithotomy-position procedures.
