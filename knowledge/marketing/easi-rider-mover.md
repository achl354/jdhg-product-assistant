# Easi Rider & Easi Mover — Motorised Trolley Tugs

**Content type: marketing/sales collateral (JDHG flyer), not a manufacturer IFU or ARTG/regulatory document.**

## What they are

Australian designed and manufactured, battery-powered motorised trolley/bin tugs, eliminating manual-handling risk when moving heavy trolleys, linen carts, meal trolleys, and wheelie bins. Both rated **1500kg pulling capacity** (less on gradients — refer to Operator Manual), safe on ramps up to ±7° gradient.

## Easi Mover — Pedestrian Tug/Bin Mover
- Product code: OHRI-D009-A009NH
- Tiller throttle controls forward/backward movement; adjustable handle height
- Dimensions: 675mm L x 470mm W, height 800–1175mm (adjustable)
- 24V DC motor, rechargeable batteries with LED indicator; overnight (8hr) charge cycle
- Safety: emergency stop, automatic brakes, horn, key lockout, back-off button
- Speed: 5km/h; external battery charger supplied

## Easi Rider — Ride On Tug
- Product code: OHRI-D004-A004NH
- Multi-directional joystick control for 360° movement (forwards, reverse, sideways)
- Dimensions: 759mm L x 680mm W, total height 1160mm, platform height 120mm
- 24V DC motor, rechargeable batteries with LED indicator; overnight (8hr) charge cycle
- Safety: emergency stop, automatic brakes, horn, key lockout, electronically lockable controller, **Bluetooth connectivity** to smartphone
- Speed: 5km/h max; compact footprint fits through standard doors; low centre of gravity
- External battery charger supplied

## Towing hitches (for both tugs)
- **Pin Hitch** — product code OHRI-D004-B0034; 22mm Ø stainless steel pin (customisable), 85mm length, R-pin safety, 1500kg towing capacity; mounted pin-up or pin-down depending on trolley tow hitch type; for linen trolleys, hospital meal trolleys, trolleys with hinged tow bar, 660L/1100L wheelie bins, stores trolleys
- **Height-Adjustable Hitch** — standard rotation OHRI-D004-B0035 (55° left/right) or extended rotation OHRI-D004-B0054 (75° left/right); manually adjustable jaw opening 20–40mm (customisable), jaw width 140mm, max attachment height 330mm above floor, 1500kg towing capacity, epoxy-coated steel jaw; for linen trolleys, hospital meal trolleys, stores & workshop trolleys
