# AMIGO Patient Escort Mover (PEM)

**Content type: marketing/sales collateral (JDHG flyer), not a manufacturer IFU or ARTG/regulatory document.**

## What it is

Attendant-driven motorised patient escort mover, designed for transporting bariatric patients over extended distances (ramps, external footpaths, sealed roads within a hospital precinct) with minimal caregiver effort.

## Features
- Solid powder-coated steel frame, upholstered seat and back cushions
- PU-cushioned armrests swing up out of the way for unobstructed patient access
- Seat frame swivels 90° in both directions (when released) for easier patient access
- Foot-activated parking brake
- 24V battery-powered traction drive, large solid rubber dual drive wheels + single rear swivel wheel, designed for uneven surfaces
- Ergonomic drive controls on operator handle behind backrest; two pressure-sensitive hand throttle levers (one each side) control forward/reverse and acceleration
- Electromagnetic brake auto-engages when no throttle pressure detected; "freewheeling mode" lever allows manual movement if the drive system malfunctions (disengages the electromagnetic brake)
- Control panel: on/off key switch, battery level gauge, horn, Hi/Lo power output buttons, diagnostic LEDs
- Receptacle for medical records below operator handle; retractable seatbelt; removable IV pole; oxygen cylinder holder; generously sized footrest platform
- External battery charger included
- **Safe Working Load: 340kg**
