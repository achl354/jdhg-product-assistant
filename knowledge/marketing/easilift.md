## What it is

EasiLift™ is an air-assisted patient lift designed to assist floor recovery following falls or floor-level incidents — supporting patient elevation from the floor to a seated or transfer-ready height. Positioned within DMG's "EasiSystem" portfolio as the floor-recovery workflow product (alongside EasiMove for lateral transfer, EasiAir for air supply).

## Key features (marketing framing)

- **Single inflation port** — one blower connection, framed as reducing set-up steps versus "Multi-Inflation Valve Air Patient Lifts" (a general product category referenced for comparison, not one specific named competitor)
- **Automatic sequential inflation** — lift chambers inflate in sequence without requiring multiple manual valve changes
- **Supportive lift profile** — designed to help cradle the patient while being elevated from floor level to a seated/transfer-ready height
- **Point-of-care access to IFU and cleaning instructions** — QR-code based, per the comparison sheet (though no actual IFU document itself was available for this ingestion — see NEEDS CONFIRMATION note above)
- Compatible with EasiAir™ and HoverTech® HT-Air® 2300 air supplies. **"Planned EasiAir GO compatibility"** is explicitly described as a future/roadmap item (battery-operated, portability-focused) — do not present EasiAir GO compatibility as available today.
