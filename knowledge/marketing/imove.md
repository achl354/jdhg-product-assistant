# I-MOVE — Patient Transfer Chair Range

**Content type: marketing/sales collateral (JDHG flyers), not a manufacturer IFU or ARTG/regulatory document.** Manufactured in France; "200 years of healthcare-sector heritage" and other manufacturer positioning claims are as stated in JDHG's marketing material, not independently verified.

## Range overview
Three configurations on one shared platform: **I-MOVE Stackable** (manual), **I-MOVE EZ-GO** (motorised), **I-MOVE EZ-GO Bariatric** (motorised, wider/higher-capacity).

## Shared design across the range
- Epoxy-coated steel frame with shock protection; high-resilience (HR) foam upholstery (35kg/m³); seamless vinyl cover for easy cleaning
- Swing-up armrests for unobstructed lateral transfers; integrated patient file holder (no bending during transfers)
- Rear wheels Ø300mm (cushioning band), swivel front wheels Ø125mm
- 95% recyclable at end of life (metal infinitely recyclable, plastic recycled on-site, wood recovered for biomass; motors/wheels excepted); vegetal-fibre panels from 100% reclaimed FSC-certified wood
- Named features: **EasyClip®** (tool-free accessory attach/adjust/remove), **EasyClean®** (seamless surfaces for fast cleaning/infection control), **EasyMov'** (shock absorbers reduce push/steer effort), **SoftSound®** (anti-vibration supports targeting ~20dB noise reduction)
- Claimed 10-year service life

## I-MOVE Stackable
Everyday manual stackable transfer chair.
- Product code: CFBM-TRS-SV
- Ergonomic foot braking (red pedal = brake, green = release); swing-up armrest; retractable footrest
- Stackable: 5 chairs reclaim 56% floor space, 10 chairs reclaim up to 64%
- Optional floor-stand locking system with anti-theft chain
- **SWL 200kg**, max patient weight 135kg, structure weight 39kg
- Dimensions: 109cm L x 75cm W x 100cm H; seat H55 x D45 x W46cm

## I-MOVE EZ-GO — Motorised
- Product code: CFBM-EZTRSV2
- Motorised traction via independent drive wheel + onboard battery with external charger; rotating handle for progressive acceleration; two forward speeds + one reverse
- Up to 20km range per battery charge (a major upgrade over the previous ~5km-range model); lockable key-secured battery; active braking (brakes when powered on, rolls freely when off); charge indicators on controller and battery; continuously variable speed
- Ships with one battery + charger; second battery optional
- **SWL 200kg**, max patient weight 135kg, structure weight 56kg
- Dimensions: 109cm L x 75cm W x 100cm H; seat H55 x D45 x W46cm; 5cm HR foam upholstery

## I-MOVE EZ-GO Bariatric — Motorised
- Product code: CFBM-EZTRSOv2 (also referenced as CFBM-EZTSROv2 in one source document — confirm exact code with JDHG before quoting)
- Wider 66cm seat, welded bariatric upholstery (cleaner/more hygienic than seamed), 8cm HR foam upholstery
- Same motorised drive/control features as standard EZ-GO
- **SWL 250kg**, max patient weight 185kg, structure weight 56kg
- Dimensions: 109cm L x 86cm W x 100cm H; seat H59 x D47 x W66cm

## Options & accessories (all models)
Head rest, accessory basket (included with motorised EZ-GO models), hooks for bags, oxygen-cylinder holder, leg-rest equipment (standard and bariatric), U-shape anti-theft fitting, anti-static system, coin acceptor, removable IV pole (2- or 4-hook), adjustable leg rest, safety belt, floor stand with chair-locking system, additional battery (EZ-GO models), wall fixation, engraved ID plate.
