# NetZero — Sustainable Underpads & Curtains

**Content type: marketing/sales collateral (JDHG flyers), not a manufacturer IFU or ARTG/regulatory document.** Treat specs and sustainability claims below as JDHG marketing material rather than independently verified clinical or regulatory fact.

## NetZero Underpad

Single-use underpad positioned as a sustainable alternative to traditional plastic-backed disposable underpads.

- **Backing:** eco-friendly bioplastic (cornstarch-based bioplastic) backing, instead of traditional plastic
- **Claimed benefits:** superior absorbency (reduces leakage), single-use hygiene, reduced caregiver changes, lower carbon footprint and reduced toxic emissions on incineration vs. traditional plastic-backed underpads
- **Sustainability framing (marketing claim, not independently verified):** cites that traditional disposable plastic-backed underpads amount to ~750,000,000 units disposed globally each year (~2,000 tonnes to landfill), and that a single Australian health service consumes ~440,000 units annually
- **Disposal:** if unsoiled, dispose in green/compostable waste stream where available; if soiled or no green-waste stream available, general waste; do not incinerate as first choice

## NetZero Curtain

Single-use privacy curtain made with **recycled content** and **fully recyclable**, positioned as a lower-carbon alternative to traditional virgin-polypropylene disposable curtains.

- **Compliance claim:** antimicrobial and fire-retardant treated (AS 2755.2–1985)
- Available in a paediatric print in addition to standard colours
- **Sustainability framing (marketing claim):** recycled polypropylene claimed to produce over 40% fewer carbon emissions than virgin plastic

### Curtain types & sizes
- **Standard – Eyelet, 2m drop** — 2.5m wide (box of 15), 4.5m wide (box of 8), 7.5m wide (box of 5)
- **Perforated – Eyelet, 2.5m drop** — 2.5m wide (box of 15), 4.5m wide (box of 6), 7.5m wide (box of 4)
- **Shower curtain with Mouldguard, 2.5m wide**
