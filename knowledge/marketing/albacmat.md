# AlbacMat — Emergency Evacuation Rescue Mat

**Content type: marketing collateral (JDHG flyer) plus one manufacturer user manual (AlbacMat Manual, 2019) — the manual is closer to IFU-grade documentation than the rest of this folder, but is dated 2019 and should be checked for currency against any newer manufacturer revision before being treated as fully authoritative.**

## What it is

Patent No. 2011250700. A rescue/evacuation mat for moving frail or immobile persons from life-threatening situations (fire, medical emergency) by sliding rather than lifting/carrying. Manufactured by Albac Enterprises Pty Ltd (Australia).

## Design & materials
- Corrugated polypropylene board base — slides easily over carpet (incl. Flotex/Uger), lino, timber, and outdoor surfaces (gravel, bitumen, concrete); crushing point 4.92 kgf/cm²
- Rolls up to 30cm x 7cm for storage; fluorescent strips and instruction label for use by untrained bystanders
- 3 sets of Velcro straps (torso, hips/can form X over torso, shins) plus a foot holder to keep feet contained during transit, especially on stairs
- Independently tested by CSIRO (Textiles division): stitching breaking point >500kg; very slow/no ignition in flammability testing
- Meets AS 2755.2 and AS 2001.2.3.2, CE marked (Class 1)
- **Safe Working Load: 470kg** (current flyer); the 2019 manual separately states an SWL of 500kg with a tested safe load of 160kg — flag this discrepancy to a rep and confirm current rating with JDHG/Regulatory rather than quoting one number as definitive

## Range
- **AlbacMat Rescue Mat (Regular)** — 193cm x 64cm — product code FEAE-AMB-66
- **AlbacMat Rescue Mat (Large)** — 193cm x 94cm — product code FEAE-AMB-90 (also usable in non-slip-floor areas indoors, e.g. operating theatres; carried by up to 8 people for outdoor rescue)
- **AlbacMat Holder** (holds 4 mats of either size) — product code FEAE-AMH
- Variants mentioned in the manual but not in the current flyer: Bariatric size (based on the Large, wider base board) and a version with Thermal Cover (larger at torso, 3 carry handles per side) — contact JDHG/local distributor for current availability

## Use technique (per manual)
- Correct posture: step forward first (raises patient into position with straight back, bent elbows), stay close to the mat (gravity/leverage)
- Single-person operation is standard; two-person technique recommended for larger/difficult patients or stairs
- Stairs: different technique for single vs. two-person descent (backward-angled single-person descent using under-handles; two-person descent with one person stationed on the stairs); ascending stairs is possible but not ideal, best with two people
- Maintenance: unroll and inspect every 6 months (straps, stitching, board grazing); clean with hose/cloth, air-dry, avoid harsh chemicals; replace if stitching is worn/broken (do not re-stitch) or board grazing is significant
- Markets referenced: aged care/nursing homes, hospitals (esp. theatre rooms with the thermal-cover variant), mining, mortuary transfers, schools, cruise ships, emergency services (ambulance/fire/SES in Victoria), general public
