## EasiMove PRO — reusable lateral transfer
*Source: DMG-EMPRO-FLY-001, V1.0, JDHG170626*

Positioned for facilities wanting to reduce reliance on single-use transfer products while keeping familiar air-assisted handling practice. Marketing framing highlights: reduced single-use/consumable reliance; heat-sealed seams (fewer stitch-line/needle-hole concerns vs. sewn construction); wipe-clean, launderable design; visual positioning cues (head position indicator, centre line); compatible with EasiAir™ and HoverTech® HT-Air® 2300 air supplies. Specs (34"/39", 453kg SWL, RF-welded, nylon twill) match the manufacturer IFU.

## EasiMove SPU — single-patient-use lateral transfer
*Source: DMG-EMSPU-FLY-001, V1.0, JDHG170626*

Available in Standard (34"/39"/50") and Split-Leg (34") configurations. Marketing framing highlights: "measured transfer performance" and "full-width coverage" claims — both carry an asterisked footnote stating they're **based on internal comparative testing under controlled conditions, and results may vary** with patient weight, surface type, transfer technique, air supply, mattress size, and clinical environment — treat as a DMG-reported claim, not independently verified. Visual positioning cues (head position indicator, centre line) and the Split-Leg workflow option for lower-body/theatre/imaging access are also highlighted. Specs match the manufacturer IFU (453kg SWL across all sizes).

## Sustainability / recycled packaging
*Source: EasiMove SPU recycled-packaging marketing flyer/FAQ (filename dated "23AUG," year not confirmed in the source filename itself)*

- Each EasiMove SPU is packaged in a compostable bag made of **PLA + PBAT** bioplastic (derived from renewable resources such as corn starch), instead of conventional fossil-based plastic packaging
- The EasiMove SPU flyer (above) separately claims **"GRS-certified recycled content"** in the product's own material (>50% recycled content per the comparison sheet in `competitive-positioning-easimove.md`) and compostable PLA/PBAT packaging — these are two distinct sustainability claims (recycled product material vs. compostable packaging), both DMG/JDHG-reported, not independently audited by JDHG
- **Composting requires industrial facilities** with specific conditions (55–70°C, 50–60% moisture, sufficient oxygen) — PLA is not necessarily home-compostable, and breaks down within 1–6 months only under those industrial conditions
- If a PLA recycling stream or industrial compost facility isn't available, incineration is presented as an effective fallback waste-management option — the source frames incineration of PLA as producing minimal toxic by-products and no microplastic formation, since it fully breaks down the material (this is a marketing claim, not an independently verified environmental assessment)
- Disposal guidance (general, not from this specific flyer): if unsoiled, dispose per local green/compostable waste stream where available; if soiled or no such stream is available, general waste

Treat all sustainability/environmental claims in this section as JDHG/DMG-reported marketing claims rather than independently verified environmental fact, consistent with how sustainability claims are handled for NetZero and Medsalv elsewhere in this knowledge base.
