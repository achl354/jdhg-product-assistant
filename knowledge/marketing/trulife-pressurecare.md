# Trulife Pressurecare — OR Positioning & Pressure Relief Gel/Foam Range

**Content type: marketing/sales collateral (JDHG booklets, posters, flyers), not a manufacturer IFU or ARTG/regulatory document.** Test results cited below (disinfectant, flammability, autoclave, temperature) are as reported by Trulife/JDHG marketing material; treat as manufacturer-reported rather than independently verified by JDHG unless a rep needs a formal test certificate, in which case refer them to JDHG/Regulatory.

## Company background
Trulife is an international group manufacturing niche healthcare products (pressurecare, orthopaedics, prosthetics, breastcare, wheelchair cushions). Began pressure-relieving OR products in 1995; JD Healthcare Group is the Australian distributor.

## Why pressurecare matters (marketing framing, citing external sources)
Surgical patients account for an estimated 20–30% of all pressure injuries (cited: surgicalproductsmag.com, 2010). Prevalence of pressure ulcers cited by country: Germany 21.1%, Australia 28.2%, Italy 27%, US 11.5%, Canada 16.5% (various sources cited in the booklet, dated ~2006–2009 studies — note these are old citations, not current Australian data). Areas most at risk: head, elbows, sacrum, heels, shoulders, hips, buttocks, knees.

## Product range/material tiers
- **Azure** — two independent protective silicone layers combined for more contoured, less structured conformity; positioned as the most advanced protection against decubitus ulcers, nerve damage, skin breakdown
- **Oasis** — the original range: 100% reusable silicone gel pads, softer than skin/tissue, pressure relief via slight gel movement/dissipation
- **Oasis Elite** — silicone gel + lightweight foam, pre-contoured surface, >50% lighter than comparable gel-only products, designed for easier handling by OR staff
- **Oasis Plus** — medical-grade silicone elastomer, no outer film layer (added durability), anatomical + hypoallergenic + anti-slip design; described as the brand's most advanced range

General material properties claimed across the range: redistributes weight, performs like human skin, easily cleaned, reusable, shock-absorbing, can be heated to 40°C or cooled to -12°C, hypoallergenic, easily repaired if cut, fire-rated self-extinguishing, does not harden over time, retains shape after deformation, UV-resistant.

## Testing (manufacturer/JDHG-reported)
- **Disinfectant compatibility:** tested against a range of hospital disinfectant chemistries (phenolics, quaternary ammonia, quaternary ammonia/alcohol blends) — most common hospital disinfectants tested as compatible (see source flyer for the specific product-by-product pass list); do **not** soak Oasis/Oasis+/Elite/Azure pads in cleaning solution overnight, as this may affect long-term durability
- **Flammability:** tested by BTTG Fire Technology Services (UK) to BS 7175 Section 2 — fire-rated as self-extinguishing "as received"
- **Autoclave sterilisation (Oasis Plus range only):** 24 autoclave cycles at 134°C for 3 minutes showed no discernible change in appearance, degradation, or contamination, and no change in function/performance (pre/post lifecycle and pressure-map tested); cycle count based on assumed max of once/month over a 2-year warranty
- **Temperature:** takes a minimum of 30 minutes to heat from 20°C to 50°C in a preheated oven; will not return to room temperature for at least 3 hours once heated (varies with room/patient temperature); pads may be preheated to 40°C or to body temperature via oven or heating mattress, following the oven/mattress manufacturer's instructions
