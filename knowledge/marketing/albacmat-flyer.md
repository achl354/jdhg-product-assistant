## What it is

Emergency evacuation rescue mat, manufactured by Albac Enterprises Pty Ltd (Australia), for moving frail or immobile persons from life-threatening situations (fire, medical emergency) by sliding rather than lifting/carrying.

## Features (per this flyer)
- Slides over all types of surfaces (stairs, carpet, concrete)
- Single-person operation in most situations
- Use in any location — independent of environment (bed, bathroom, corridor)
- Ready for instant use — compact, self-storage, lightweight
- 3 patient securing straps, 3 transit straps, 1 transit strap, 6 carry handles (as listed on this flyer)
- Thermal cover — for warmth/protection, can also help calm a panicky patient
- Polypropylene board base — patient comfort, heat protection, slide-ability
- Available in 2 sizes: Regular (193cm x 64cm) and Large (193cm x 94cm)
- Optional accessory: AlbacMat Holder (holds 4 AlbacMats)

## Safe Working Load — NEEDS CONFIRMATION
**This flyer states 470kg Safe Working Load.** The manufacturer's 2019 user manual (`marketing/albacmat-manual-2019.md`) separately states a 500kg SWL with a 160kg tested safe load. Nothing in either document explains the difference (revision change vs. a different testing basis), and no newer manufacturer document was available to resolve it. **Do not quote either figure as the definitive current SWL without confirming with JDHG/Regulatory first** — tell the rep this explicitly if asked.
