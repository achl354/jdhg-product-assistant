# Medsalv — Remanufactured Single-Use Device Program

**Content type: marketing/sales collateral (JDHG flyers), not a manufacturer IFU or ARTG/regulatory document.**

## What it is

A joint sustainability program between JD Healthcare Group and Medsalv: hospitals send used single-use patient-transfer devices (HoverMatt SPU, EasiMove SPU) to Medsalv for remanufacturing instead of landfill, then receive a blended supply of new + remanufactured devices back at a lower cost than new-only.

## How it works
1. **Collection** — hospital places used devices in Medsalv collection points; collected free of charge by Medsalv, no sorting required
2. **Remanufacturing** — devices remanufactured at Medsalv's ISO 13485-accredited facility (Geelong, Australia), with unique identifiers applied for tracking; every device functionally tested
3. **Ordering** — hospital orders using the blended product codes below via their normal ordering process (only available to hospitals participating in collection)
4. **Returning** — JD Healthcare Group sends a blend of new + remanufactured devices in a reusable carton

## Key claims (Medsalv/distributor-reported, not independently audited by JDHG)
- Remanufactured devices make up 75% of a blended-code order (provided enough have been collected)
- Blended-code pricing is lower than ordering new-only, at the same list price point to the hospital
- Program reduces waste to landfill, carbon emissions (scope 3/4), and supports a local Australian supply chain and social-enterprise employment
- Medsalv is described as the highest-scoring B-Corp internationally as a medical supplier, a registered Social Enterprise, and certified Climate Positive
- Devices receive a Medsalv ARTG entry when remanufactured (i.e. remanufactured devices carry their own ARTG listing distinct from the new-device ARTG entry — confirm the specific ARTG number with Medsalv/Regulatory if a rep needs it, as it is not stated in this flyer)
- Cited claim: the health sector generates ~7% of Australia's carbon emissions (per Malik et al., The Lancet Planetary Health, 2018); remanufacturing claimed to reduce waste "by up to 92%" per Medsalv's internal remanufacturing data
