# EasiMove SPU — Single Patient Use Air-Assisted Lateral Transfer Mattress (Manufacturer IFU)

**Content type: manufacturer Instructions for Use (IFU), current manufacturer document.** Source: DirectMed Group Pty Ltd, "EasiMoveSPU™ Instructions for Use," Document MHDM-EMSPU, **Rev A (16/06/2026, initial release)** — company SharePoint copy; no later revision of this specific document was found at ingestion time.

## What it is

The EasiMoveSPU™ is a **single-patient-use** air-assisted lateral transfer mattress. When connected to a compatible air supply, air releases through the underside of the mattress creating a low-friction interface, assisting caregivers with lateral transfer, repositioning and boosting between adjacent support surfaces. The device does not independently move, support, or lift the patient — it must always be used with appropriate patient handling technique and facility protocols. For professional healthcare use only.

**Split-Leg (SL) models:** certain EasiMoveSPU models incorporate a Split-Leg configuration with separable lower leg sections secured by integrated fasteners. Secured, it functions as a standard EasiMoveSPU mattress; released, the lower leg sections can be positioned independently for procedures requiring leg separation, including lithotomy positioning.

Compatible air supply units: EasiAir™ Air Supply; also HoverTech® HT-Air® 2300 Air Supply.

## Indications for use

Reduced mobility; injury or illness; anaesthesia or sedation; bariatric handling requirements; clinical procedures requiring assisted patient movement; bed-to-bed, bed-to-stretcher, and imaging/operating-table transfers; patient repositioning and boosting; lithotomy and split-leg table procedures (Split-Leg models only).

Intended care environments: acute care hospitals, emergency departments, ICU, operating theatres, recovery units, imaging departments, ambulance/patient transport, aged care facilities where clinically appropriate.

## Contraindications

May not be suitable for: patients exceeding the Safe Working Load; patients who cannot be safely positioned for transfer; situations where transfer surfaces cannot be safely aligned/secured; patients with unstable spinal injuries unless approved by clinical assessment. Clinical judgement should always be exercised prior to use.

## Key warnings

- Minimum two caregivers during transfer (more depending on patient size/condition/facility policy); never leave a patient unattended while inflated
- Do not commence transfer until the mattress is fully inflated; do not use for vertical lifting
- **Do not exceed the Safe Working Load of 453 kg**
- Use only compatible air supply units; do not use if damaged, punctured, or excessively worn
- Patient Safety Straps must not be used as lifting points
- **Split-Leg models specifically:** ensure all Split-Leg fasteners are securely connected before inflation and before transfer; do not perform lateral transfers while leg sections are separated; separate leg sections only when the mattress is fully deflated; reconnect all fasteners before any subsequent transfer

## Product overview / key features

Head Position Indicator; Patient Safety Straps; Integrated Transfer Handles; Centre Alignment Line; Product Label with QR code (links to IFU); Inflation Port Connections; Low-Friction Underside; Split-Leg Fasteners (SL models only). Radiolucent construction. **This is single-patient-use only** — unlike EasiMove PRO, it is not designed for multi-patient reuse (see `easimove-pro-usage-and-ifu.md` for the reusable version).

## Cleaning and disinfection

**For use by a single patient only** — may remain with the same patient throughout an episode of care but must not be transferred between patients. Between uses (same patient): inspect for damage/contamination, remove visible soil, wipe external surfaces with approved hospital disinfectants per the disinfectant manufacturer's instructions. If heavily contaminated, damaged, or no longer suitable for use, remove from service and dispose of per facility policy.

**Do not:** machine wash, launder, autoclave, steam clean, or sterilise.

## Storage, disposal, and reporting

Store in a clean, dry environment away from direct sunlight, moisture, and excessive heat; do not use if packaging is damaged or contaminated. Dispose per facility policy and local waste management requirements — treat as clinical waste if contaminated with blood/body fluids. Any serious incident should be reported to DirectMed Group Pty Ltd and the relevant regulatory authority per local requirements.
