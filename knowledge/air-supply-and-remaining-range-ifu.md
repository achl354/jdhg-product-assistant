## HT-Air 2300 Air Supply

**Intended use:** provides 6 airflow options to inflate HoverTech's air-assisted transfer/lift/positioning devices. Operator (caregiver) is the intended user — not the patient.

**Precautions:** never leave patient unattended on an inflated device; use only HoverTech-authorized attachments (unauthorized use may injure the patient, malfunction, and void the warranty); MRI use needs the 25 ft specialty MRI hose; do not open the unit (electric shock risk); must connect to a supply main with protective earth; **not compatible with DC power supplies; not for use with the HoverJack Battery Cart**; not for use near flammable anesthetics, in a hyperbaric chamber, or oxygen tent.

**Specs:** 31.75 x 17.8 x 17.8 cm, 5.67kg. European model HTAIR2300: 230Vac, 50Hz, 6A. Class I equipment, Type BF applied part. Operating temp 10-40°C, humidity 10-70% non-condensing. 5-year service life. **No user-serviceable parts** — only qualified service personnel may repair it.

**Cleaning:** wipe down with EPA-approved hospital-grade disinfectant wipes/spray-on-cloth between patients (never spray liquid directly onto the unit — may degrade control panel graphics over time; replacement panels available from HoverTech).

**Preventive maintenance:** visually inspect the power cord for fraying/nicks before use. Air filters (either side of the motor) should be assessed per facility schedule or annually — remove screws, wash under warm water, dry fully before replacing; replace if debris won't wash out or if the filter loses shape/deteriorates.

**Infection control:** for isolation patients, apply the facility's standard equipment protocol; filters can be removed and disinfected/replaced between airborne-isolation patients. A disposable air hose cover (Model ASHC, box of 25) is available.

## Q2Roller (Lateral Turning Device)

**Indications:** patients unable to turn/position themselves; Q2 turning for pressure off-loading; post-prone positioning (in lieu of pillows/foam/gel); assisting a patient to sit on the edge of the bed.

**Contraindications:** unstable thoracic, cervical, or lumbar fractures.

**Precautions:** minimum two caregivers recommended (one caregiver may operate it alone only if side rails are raised); ensure valves don't contact the patient; single-patient use, trained personnel only; center the patient using the Reference Center Line Indicator before inflating; patient must be attended/stabilized during inflation, deflation, or whenever inflated past 30 degrees; **do not launder**.

**Step-by-step — lateral turn:**
1. Cap the red deflation valves tightly before inflating.
2. Place on the bed mattress, center using the Center Line Indicator, tuck the securing apron under the mattress.
3. Center an absorbent pad on top (or place device+pad under an already-positioned patient via log-roll).
4. Confirm patient centered, bed/stretcher rails up (or per facility protocol).
5. Place the air hose over the inflation valve on the OPPOSITE side of the intended turn direction; press ADJUSTABLE on the HT-Air keypad (up to 4 presses to increase flow).
6. When the turn is achieved, remove hose, press STANDBY (chamber doesn't need to be fully inflated to reach the desired position).
7. To deflate quickly: remove the red cap from the deflation valve. To deflate slowly/adjust: depress the center of the black inflation valve.

**Post-prone positioning:** follow steps 1-4 above, then prone the patient onto the device (do not use the Q2Roller to perform the proning maneuver itself — only for post-prone offloading), inflate/adjust chambers per facility protocol.

**Sitting a patient on the edge of the bed** (minimum 2 caregivers, clinical judgement required): follow turning steps 1-4, fully inflate the chamber opposite the eventual seated side to turn the patient (bend knees for comfort), raise then lower the bed head so feet reach the floor when seated, slide legs to the bed edge while inflating the other chamber to prevent sliding off, then once seated and fully inflated, stabilize the patient and open the deflate valve while guiding legs/stabilizing as it deflates.

**Specs:** polyurethane-laminated non-woven/nylon non-woven, RF-welded. Chambers 46.5"x44.5" (118x113cm), apron 72"x44" (183x112cm). Model HTR-200. **Weight limit 600 lbs / 272 kg.** Single-patient use, latex-free.

**Cleaning:** wipe down with germicidal cleaner (phenolic/quaternary/intermediate-level disinfectant per facility procedure); apply into hard-to-reach areas; air dry; **do not launder**.

## HoverMatt T-Burg (Trendelenburg Patient Stabilization & Air-Transfer Mattress)

**Intended use:** surgical procedures placing the patient in Trendelenburg/Reverse Trendelenburg up to 40°, including with robotics. Highly breathable open-cell design lets it stay under the patient after surgery.

**Indications:** Trendelenburg/Reverse Trendelenburg positioning (with or without lithotomy/tilt); patients unable to assist in lateral transfer/repositioning/boosting.

**Contraindications:** **patients over 400 lbs (182 kg)**; unstable thoracic/cervical/lumbar fractures unless cleared by facility clinical decision.

**Precautions — Trendelenburg positioning:** connecting straps must be tightly connected to the OR table so the HoldFast strip contacts the table; attach patient safety straps across the patient; **the patient's skin must make direct contact with the HoldFast surface**; **do not exceed 40°**.

**Precautions — OR-to-PACU transfer:** verify caster brakes engaged; **minimum three caregivers** for lateral transfers; never leave patient unattended while inflated; never transfer on an uninflated T-Burg; low-air-loss bed transfers need highest airflow setting.

**Step-by-step — transfer from PACU to OR table:**
1. Patient supine, centered on the T-Burg with buttocks aligned to the perineum cutout.
2. Surfaces as close as possible, lock all wheels.
3. Plug in Air Supply, insert hose nozzle at foot end, turn on.
4. Push at an angle (head or feet first); receiving-side caregiver grasps handles halfway across and pulls into position; foot-end caregiver guides the patient's feet.
5. Center patient/mat on the OR table's perineum cutout before deflation; turn off Air Supply.
6. Proceed to attach to the OR table (below). **Always use a minimum of three caregivers.**

**Step-by-step — attaching to the OR table:**
1. Start with the table flat, no tilt; confirm the OR table pad is properly attached.
2. Place the T-Burg on the table; center the grey HoldFast foam and perineum cutout on the table (T-Burg's cutout sits slightly above the table's own cutout).
3. Tuck the sides under the OR table pad, HoldFast strip flat against the table.
4. Attach via Method 1 (loop rail fasteners behind the nearest rail post) or Method 2 (loop before/after the rail post) — either way, attach to the fastening strip; behind the post for Trendelenburg, in front for Reverse Trendelenburg.
5. Loop foot-end straps around the footboard post/stirrups/other table point.
6. Ensure the mat lies flat (no wrinkles) before positioning the patient.
7. Align the patient's buttocks close to the perineum cutout; maximize skin contact with the HoldFast foam.
8. Loop each patient safety (body) strap behind the OR table rail and back onto itself per the label, both sides; bring across the patient's chest and secure.
9. Place arm protectors (purchased separately) around each arm per hospital protocol.
10. Position the table/patient for the procedure. **Ensure the T-Burg is firmly attached before positioning for surgery, and follow facility protocols for Trendelenburg positioning and for periodic monitoring of skin integrity/circulation. To be used by a licensed medical professional only.**

**Step-by-step — following the procedure (OR to PACU):**
1. Return patient to supine.
2. Disconnect the T-Burg from the OR table (loosen rail-fastener and foot-end straps; reconnect footplate/headboard, remove legs from stirrups if used); untuck any portion tucked under the table pad.
3. Plug in Air Supply, insert hose into a foot-end port, lock all wheels, prefer high-to-low surface transfer.
4. Turn on, push at an angle headfirst, receiving caregiver pulls into position (foot-end caregiver guides feet).
5. Center before deflation, turn off, raise rails.

**Specs:** top breathable open-cell elastomeric foam, bottom high-MVTR nylon twill, sewn. 34" x 45" (86x114cm). Model HM-TBURG-34 (and -KIT with arm protectors). **Weight limit 400 lbs / 181 kg.** Single-patient use.

**Cleaning:** disposable, single-patient use — **not to be laundered**. Visual inspection before use (straps, handles, no tears/holes preventing inflation) — remove from use if damaged.

## SitAssist Pro (Positioning Device)

**Intended use:** pneumatically lifts a patient's upper body from supine to upright seated (and controllably back down) without manual effort. For patients needing mid-to-moderate assistance. Radiolucent, MRI-compatible.

**Care settings:** hospitals, long-term/extended care, diagnostic/radiology centers (X-ray, MRI, CT, fluoroscopy, ultrasound).

**Contraindications:** unstable thoracic/cervical/lumbar fractures unless cleared by facility clinical decision.

**Precautions:** clinical assessment by a qualified professional before use; center the patient before inflating/deflating; **always support the patient's head when sitting up or lying down**; patient must be attended/stabilized while the device is inflated; more than one caregiver may be necessary; trained personnel only. For "totally dependent" patients, a dedicated caregiver is required in addition to whoever manages the device/air supply. **Use with the HT-Air unit only — do not use Air200G or Air400G.**

**Step-by-step — sitting a patient upright:**
1. Center the device on the surface, logo up; confirm both deflation valves closed.
2. Center the patient using the centering line, align hips to the device's lower edge using the hip placement line.
3. If used near imaging equipment, fold wings inward and secure the patient with a standard strap if needed.
4. After imaging (if applicable), insert the hose into an inflation valve near the patient's hip, secure the snap.
5. Press ADJUSTABLE up to 4 times to gradually inflate; once patient is confirmed centered, fully inflate via the 34" HoverMatt button, supporting the patient's head as they move upright.
6. Press STANDBY when fully inflated; remove hose.
7. To deflate after the patient is removed: open the red quick-deflate valve cap.

**Step-by-step — laying a patient down:**
1. Center the device, logo up, both deflation valves closed (log-roll under an already-positioned patient if needed, apron extended flat).
2. Insert hose into an inflation valve near the hip, secure the snap.
3. Press the 34" HoverMatt button to inflate; press STANDBY once fully inflated, remove hose if needed.
4. Center the patient using the centering/hip lines.
5. Deflate to lower from seated to supine, **supporting the patient's head throughout**: open the clear deflate valve + depress the inside flap for a slow/gradual deflation, or open the red quick-deflate cap for rapid deflation.

**Specs:** 210D nylon tarpaulin / clear ether TPU double-laminated, RF-welded. 48" x 53.5" with apron (121x136cm). Model SAP-200. **Weight limit 600 lbs / 272 kg.** Required accessory: HTAIR1200/2300 air supply; optional 25 ft MRI hose (HTA-MRI).

**Cleaning:** wipe down with hospital disinfection solution, 10:1 bleach, or disinfectant wipes between patients (bleach may discolor fabric). **Do not launder.**

## EMS Evacuation HoverJack

**Intended use:** lifts a patient (supine) from floor to bed/stretcher height, and can additionally evacuate patients up/down stairs in an emergency.

**Indications:** patients unable to assist their own vertical lift (e.g. after a fall); CPR compatible when fully inflated.

**Contraindications:** unstable thoracic/cervical/lumbar fractures unless cleared by facility clinical decision.

**Care settings:** hospitals, long-term/extended care, patient transport services, and EMS.

**Precautions:** secure patient safety straps only after full inflation, never before; move it using transport straps/handles, never the patient straps (may tear); move slowly and cautiously once inflated; additional caregivers recommended over 350 lbs/159 kg; never move a patient on an uninflated unit; never leave a patient unattended while inflated.

**Warnings:** minimum two caregivers for inflation/transport; **minimum three caregivers for stairwell evacuation.**

**Two hardware versions** (same as standard HoverJack): pre-June 2023 separate inflate/deflate valves; post-June 2023 universal valves.

**Step-by-step — as an air patient lift:**
1. Place on the floor next to the patient — valve #4 chamber on top, valve #1 against the floor.
2. Cap/close all valves tightly before inflating (universal valves: if a valve spins freely, center, push in, turn clockwise to engage).
3. Log-roll the patient on, feet at the valve end (or transfer via HoverMatt, always deflated first).
4. Minimum two caregivers — one with the patient, one managing inflation, third (if available) on the other side.
5. Plug in and turn on the Air Supply at the foot end.
6. Inflate valve #1 first (hold hose against it); once fully inflated the chamber stays inflated on its own; remove hose.
7. Secure patient safety straps (only once inflation is far enough along per hardware version — see full manual for exact sequencing).
8. Repeat in exact succession for valves #2, #3, #4 until the desired height is reached.
9. Turn off the Air Supply; attach valve caps if desired.
10. Move to the desired location using transport handles/straps — never the patient straps.
11. To lower: open the uppermost valve first, deflate fully, then move down in succession; **never release multiple chambers at once.**
12. Close all valves once deflated, ready for next use.

**Step-by-step — as a stairwell evacuation device:**
1. Inflate per the air-patient-lift steps above (steps 1-11 of that sequence).
2. Unzip the foot pouch, position as close as possible to the adjacent surface, transfer the patient onto it.
3. Center the patient, zip the foot pouch; inflate the head-end wedge if necessary.
4. Secure safety straps over the patient snugly.
5. Using the foot-end transport straps/handles, pull the patient to the stairwell, positioned to descend feet-first.
6. **Before descending, chambers 3 and 4 must be fully deflated** — open valve #4 slowly, fully deflate, then repeat for #3. **Never release multiple chambers at once.**
7. Retighten patient safety straps after deflating chambers 3 and 4.
8. Head-end caregiver (should be the physically stronger of the team) controls the descent using head-end transport straps; two caregivers hold head-end straps/handles while the foot-end caregiver pulls the patient down using the foot-end strap — the Teflon-infused bottom slides down each flight; the foot-end caregiver may brace with their thigh to slow descent if needed.
9. **Keep the patient centered throughout, and ensure their head does not slump forward and restrict breathing during evacuation.**
10. **Minimum three caregivers for stairwell evacuation** (two at head end, one at foot end).

**Specs:** topside nylon oxford/nylon, skirt Cordura fabric, underside Teflon, RF-welded. 32" (81cm) x 72" (183cm), inflated height 30" (76cm). Model HJ32EV-2. **Weight limit: 1200 lbs / 544 kg for vertical lift; 700 lbs / 318 kg for stairwell evacuation** (note the lower limit for stair descent).

**Cleaning:** wipe down between patients with hospital disinfectant/10:1 bleach/disinfectant wipes (may discolor fabric); scrub brush may help. **Do not launder.**
