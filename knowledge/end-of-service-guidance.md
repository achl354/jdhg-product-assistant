# HoverTech Reusable Device — End-of-Service & Label Integrity Guidance

Source: JDHCG-GD-001, Version 1.0, April 2026. Issued by JD Healthcare Group (Australia's authorised distributor of HoverTech products).
Curated: 2026-07-09 — current as of this date. Reviewed annually or on product/regulatory change.

## Scope — which products this applies to
Reusable HoverTech products only:
- HoverMatt Reusable Air Transfer Mattress — models HM28HS, HM34HS, HM39HS, HM50HS. Machine wash per manufacturer IFU; air drying preferred; if dryer used, low temperature only.
- HoverMatt Double-Coated Reusable Mattress — models HM28DC, HM34DC, HM39DC, HM50DC. Must NOT be placed in a dryer.
- Reusable Positioning Wedges — wipe-down only.
- HoverJack Air Patient Lift (APL) — models HJM-32, HJM-39. Wipe-down with approved hospital-grade disinfectant between uses. Do not machine wash. Do not submerge. Air dry completely before storage.
- HoverJack Evacuation Device II — model HJEV2-32. Wipe-down with approved hospital-grade disinfectant. Do not machine wash or launder. Do not submerge. Air dry completely before storage.

**Not covered by this document:** HoverMatt SPU (Single Patient Use) products — these are never laundered; retire per the SPU IFU when soiled or compromised, not per this guidance.

## Why label integrity matters
The product label carries model number, serial/lot number, max patient weight rating, manufacturer ID, laundering/care instructions, and warning symbols. Losing this information compromises safe use, correct reprocessing, and traceability.

Regulatory basis: Schedule 1, Part 2, Clause 13 of the Therapeutic Goods (Medical Devices) Regulations 2002 (Cth) requires medical devices to carry information identifying the device/manufacturer, safe-use instructions, and legible presentation.

## Device retirement criteria — retire immediately if ANY ONE applies
**Primary trigger (independent of wash-cycle count or physical condition):**
- Label missing, fully detached, or degraded so key info (model number, serial/lot number, or weight rating) is no longer legible.

**Additional triggers:**
- Visible punctures, tears, or breaches compromising inflation/air retention
- Delamination, cracking, or separation of the polyurethane-coated or heat-sealed fabric
- Any seam separation or RF-weld failure, even minor
- Valve cap missing, damaged, or unable to form an airtight seal
- Persistent inability to inflate to operational pressure with an approved HoverTech air supply
- Visible contamination not resolved by the facility's standard decontamination protocol
- Any physical modification or repair performed by a party other than HoverTech International or JD Healthcare Group

## Wash-cycle guidance (operational guidance only — does NOT override the label-legibility trigger above; HoverTech International does not publish a manufacturer-defined maximum wash cycle count)
- 1–75 cycles: label intact and legible → continue in service, inspect label at each use
- 75–100 cycles: label showing wear → increase inspection frequency, consider proactive assessment with a JD Healthcare rep
- 100+ cycles: high risk of label degradation → conduct full condition inspection, contact JD Healthcare Group to assess replacement
- Any cycle count: label missing or illegible → retire immediately, do not return to use

## Pre-use inspection checklist (every use)
1. Label present and fully legible (model, serial/lot, weight rating) — fail → retire immediately
2. Mattress body intact, no punctures/tears — fail → remove from service
3. Heat-sealed seams and RF-welds intact, no bubbling/lifting/separation — fail → remove from service
4. Polyurethane coating undamaged, no cracking/peeling/delamination — fail → remove from service (degraded coating raises infection-control risk)
5. Valve caps present and sealing correctly — fail → replace cap or retire
6. Device inflates correctly to operational pressure and holds air — fail → remove from service
7. Free of visible contamination (staining, odour, biological material) — fail → reprocess or retire
8. Patient safety straps (if fitted) intact — fail → remove from service

## Retirement & disposal steps
1. Remove from circulation immediately, quarantine, record/photograph remaining label info, mark "RETIRED — DO NOT USE"
2. Do not repair, modify, or return to clinical use — no facility-staff repair is authorised under the HoverTech warranty/IFU
3. Contact JD Healthcare Group for replacement or fleet review (see contact below)
4. Dispose per facility waste management policy and state environmental regulations (components: nylon, polyurethane, polyester)
5. Record the retirement in the facility's medical device register / asset management system with reason and inspection findings
