## What this covers

Product: EasiMove Air-Assisted Lateral Transfer Mattress (SPU). Prepared for procurement evaluation.

## Load and transfer performance

Horizontal pull force required to initiate movement, measured across test loads and airflow settings:

| Test Load | Airflow Setting | Horizontal Pull Force to Initiate Movement |
|---|---|---|
| 300 lb (136 kg) | 1.5 m³/min (minimum) | 6.5 kg |
| 300 lb (136 kg) | 2.3 m³/min (maximum) | 7.5 kg |
| 1,200 lb (544 kg) | 2.3 m³/min (maximum) | 8 kg |

Rated Safe Working Load is 453 kg. The mattress was pull-force tested at loads up to 544 kg (≈120% of the rated SWL), where horizontal force to initiate movement remained ≤8 kg under controlled test conditions.

## Structural strength

| Property | Validated Result |
|---|---|
| Rated Safe Working Load (SWL) | 453 kg (≈998 lb) |
| Proof / overload test load | 544 kg (1,200 lb) · ≈120% of rated SWL |
| Tensile strength — fabric (horizontal) | 267 N |
| Tensile strength — fabric (longitudinal) | 411 N |
| Tensile strength — handles | 1,840 N |

## What this supports (per the document's own framing)

- Rated SWL of 453 kg — supports bariatric patient handling where required
- Tested to 544 kg (≈120% of rated SWL) with initiation force ≤8 kg under controlled conditions — margin above rating, low manual-handling effort
- Reinforced handles validated to 1,840 N tensile strength — supports secure, team-based repositioning
- Fabric tensile strength of 267 N / 411 N — supports durability across repeated use
