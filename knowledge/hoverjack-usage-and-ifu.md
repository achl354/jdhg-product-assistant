# HoverJack Air Patient Lift — Instructions for Use (Manufacturer IFU)

Source: HoverTech International official User Manual, "HJManual, Rev. J" (dated 2024-05-08). Company internal copy from SharePoint `Manuals 2024`; public manufacturer site fetch was blocked, so this is the manufacturer's own document text.
Curated: 2026-07-09.

## Intended use
In the event of a patient fall, lifts the patient in a supine position from the floor to bed/stretcher height, using the HoverTech Air Supply to inflate each of the four chambers.

## Indications
- Patients unable to assist in their own vertical lift (e.g. after a fall).
- Patients whose weight/girth poses a potential health risk to caregivers lifting/moving them.
- CPR compatible when fully inflated.

## Contraindications
- Patients experiencing unstable thoracic, cervical, or lumbar fractures should not use the HoverJack unless a clinical decision has been made by the facility.

## Intended care settings
Hospitals, long-term or extended care facilities.

## Precautions
- Do not secure patient safety straps until the HoverJack is completely inflated to the desired height.
- Confirm straps are secured before moving the patient.
- **Never use the patient safety straps to pull the HoverJack** — they may tear. Move it using the transport handles along the top perimeter instead.
- Move slowly and with caution when moving a patient on the inflated HoverJack.
- Additional caregivers recommended when moving a patient over 350 lbs / 158.76 kg.
- **Never attempt to move a patient on an un-inflated HoverJack.**
- **Never leave a patient unattended on an inflated HoverJack.**
- Only use HoverTech-authorized attachments/accessories.

## Warnings / caution
- **Minimum two caregivers** required at all times.
- One caregiver stays with the patient during inflation; the second manages inflation. A third caregiver, if available, attends the other side of the patient.

## Precautions — HoverTech Air Supply
- Not for use in the presence of flammable anesthetics, or in a hyperbaric chamber or oxygen tent.
- Route the power cord to avoid hazard; avoid blocking air intakes.
- Avoid electric shock — do not open the air supply unit.
- **Warning:** the HT-Air is not compatible with DC power supplies and is not for use with the HoverJack Battery Cart.

## Two hardware versions exist — check which one you have
- **Manufactured before 6/2023:** separate white/clear inflate valves and red deflate valves (4 of each).
- **Manufactured after 6/2023:** universal valves (4 total) — close before inflating, open to deflate, depress the center button for slower deflation.

## Step-by-step: lifting a patient (pre-June 2023 units — separate inflate/deflate valves)
1. Place the HoverJack on the floor next to the patient — chamber with valve #4 on top, chamber with valve #1 against the floor.
2. Make certain all four red-capped deflation valves are capped tightly.
3. Log-roll the patient onto the deflated HoverJack, feet at the valve end. Alternatively, place the patient on top of it using the HoverMatt (always deflate the HoverMatt before inflating the HoverJack).
4. Minimum two caregivers.
5. One caregiver stays with the patient during inflation; second manages inflation; third (if available) on the other side.
6. Plug the HoverTech Air Supply into an electrical outlet.
7. Caregiver at the foot end turns on the Air Supply.
8. Hold the hose against inlet valve #1 (closest to the floor) to begin inflation.
9. When fully inflated, remove the hose — the valve closes automatically, holding the chamber inflated.
10. Repeat in exact succession for valve #2, valve #3, valve #4, until the desired height is reached.
11. Secure patient safety straps.
12. Turn off the Air Supply; attach valve caps if desired.
13. Move the HoverJack to the desired location (using transport handles, not the straps).
14. To transfer the patient off, unbuckle the safety straps.
15. To lower the patient: open the uppermost red deflate valve (#4) first, then work downward in succession once each chamber is fully deflated.

**Chambers must be fully inflated to ensure stability. Do not release multiple chambers at once when lowering a patient.**

## Step-by-step: lifting a patient (post-June 2023 units — universal valves)
1. Place the HoverJack on the floor next to the patient — chamber with valve #4 on top, chamber with valve #1 against the floor.
2. Ensure valves are closed before inflating (if a valve spins freely when open, center it, push in, and turn clockwise to engage the threads).
3. Log-roll the patient onto the deflated HoverJack, feet at the valve end (or use the HoverMatt as above).
4. Minimum two caregivers, same inflation-attendance roles as above.
5. Plug in and turn on the Air Supply at the foot end.
6. Hold the hose against valve #1 to begin inflation; remove hose once fully inflated (chamber stays inflated).
7. Repeat in sequence for valve #2, valve #3, valve #4 until the desired height is reached.
8. Secure patient straps, turn off the Air Supply.
9. Move the HoverJack to the desired location.
10. To transfer the patient off, unbuckle the safety straps.
11. To lower: slowly open the uppermost valve; once fully deflated, move downward in succession (depress the center button for slower deflation).
12. Close all valves once deflated so it's ready for next use.

**Chambers must be fully inflated to ensure stability. Do not release multiple chambers at once when lowering a patient.**

## Specifications
- Material: topside nylon oxford, underside Teflon. RF-welded construction.
- Widths: 32" (81cm, model HJ3202T) or 39" (99cm, model HJ3902T). Length 72"-77" (183-196cm). Inflated height 30" (76cm), each chamber ~7.5" (19cm).
- **Weight limit: 1200 lbs / 544 kg.**
- Required air supply accessory: HTAIR1200 (North America), HTAIR2300 (Europe), HTAIR1000 (Japan), HTAIR2356 (Korea), or AIR200G/AIR400G canister units.

## Cleaning & maintenance
**Option 1 — wipe-down:** hospital-approved disinfection solution, 10:1 bleach solution, or disinfecting wipes between patients (may discolor fabric). Remove visible soil first, then follow the product's dwell-time/saturation guidance; a gentle scrub brush may help.
**Option 2 — full wash:** (1) ensure fully inflated with all valves/caps tightly closed; (2) scrub with soap and water (max 160°F) to remove bioburden; (3) rinse fully, keeping as much water off the valves/caps as possible; (4) stand on end, valves down, to drain, and leave to fully air-dry before storing.
**Do not launder the HoverJack in a washing machine.**

## Preventive maintenance checks
- All patient safety straps attached; transfer handles all attached; buckles intact and operational; no punctures or tears.
- Separate-valve units: inflate valves self-sealing with no leakage; deflate valves have intact red caps.
- Universal-valve units: valves open fully and close tightly. (If a valve spins freely when open, it's field-replaceable — pull straight out to remove, press firmly to re-engage until tabs click; confirm it's seated before use.)
