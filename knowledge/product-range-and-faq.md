# HoverTech Product Range — Specs & FAQ

Source: internal HoverTech product summary + training/education deck (training hub, updated June 2026).
Curated: 2026-07-09 — current as of this date.

## What the range does
Air-assisted lateral patient transfer and repositioning system. Reduces required transfer force by 80-90%, minimizing caregiver strain. Available in heat-sealed (prevents bacterial ingress) or double-coated (fluid/stain resistant) versions. Compatible with the HoverTech Air Supply for rapid inflation. Radiolucent and MRI compatible (see MRI note below).

## Product line

**HoverMatt Single-Patient Use (SPU)** — discarded after one patient to prevent cross-contamination. Sizes: 34" x 78" (86x198cm), 39" x 78" (99x198cm), 50" x 78" (127x198cm).

**HoverMatt SPU Link** — SPU with integrated Velcro straps anchoring to the bedframe for stability; used in ICU/surgical units. Sizes: 34" and 39" x 78".

**HoverMatt Mini (Half-Matt)** — compact, for confined spaces (theatre, imaging, labor/delivery). Reusable: 28" x 48" (71x122cm). SPU: 34" x 48" (86x122cm). Also see theatre/maternity variant below (114cm length, 272kg SWL, suitable for bariatric paediatric use).

**HoverMatt Split-Leg** — for lithotomy-position surgical procedures. Size: 34" x 78" (86x198cm).

**HoverSling Repositioning Sheet** — combines HoverMatt + lift sling; repositioning, chair-bed transfers, all 4 sling positions. Sizes: 39" and 50" x 78". Capacity up to 1200 lbs / 544 kg — suitable for bariatric patients. Split-Leg version: 34" (318kg SWL) or 39"/50" (454kg SWL).

**HoverJack Air Patient Lift** — inflatable floor-to-bed-height lift, 4 individually inflatable chambers, quick-release deflation valves. Weight capacity 544 kg (1200 lbs). Widths: 32" and 39". Latex-free. Industry standard for safe floor lifting; suitable with spinal board or CPR.

**HoverJack Evac II** — emergency evacuation device, stair descent capable. Weight limits: 318 kg for stair descent, 544 kg for vertical lifts. Tapered head-end with inflatable wedge pillow, steering straps, Teflon-coated removable bottom. Latex-free.

**AirWedge (Adjustable Positioning Device)** — inflatable positioning for upper-body elevation/angling; independent inflation controls. Used in ICU, emergency, post-op recovery.

**Q2Roller (Lateral Turning Device)** — two alternating inflatable chambers for gentle lateral turning; handheld inflator control; slip-resistant base. Single-patient use; 272 kg SWL. Used for hygiene/wound care access without manual lifting.

**SitAssist PRO** — powered air-assisted lifting surface to help patients rise from seated position / reposition in a chair. Reduces manual lifting/pushing/pulling for staff.

**Air Supply MHHT-AIR2300 (Variable Speed)** — portable inflation unit, variable speed settings, lightweight with handle, hose + connectors fit all HoverTech products.

## FAQ

**Is HoverMatt MRI-safe?** Snaps are nickel-plated brass — non-magnetic, MRI-safe. For MRI use, a specific 7.6-metre non-metal hose must be used to keep the air supply outside the room.

**Is it latex-free?** Yes — all HoverTech air transfer mattresses are latex-free. HoverJack and HoverJack Evac II are also latex-free.

**How long can a HoverMatt stay under a patient?** Can remain underneath for the entire hospital stay — supported by pressure studies confirming safety/effectiveness without increased pressure-injury risk.

**Cleaning/laundering?** Reusable HoverMatts: machine launder and dry (max 65°C wash; air drying preferred, never exceed 46°C in a dryer; only double-coated models must be air-dried, never machine-dried — see end-of-service-guidance.md for full retirement criteria). SPU HoverMatt: never laundered — discard if heavily soiled. All models can be cleaned with standard medical disinfectants or a 10:1 bleach solution wipe-down between patients.

**Flammability?** Both reusable and SPU HoverMatts have passed all required flammability safety testing.

**Radiolucent?** Yes — can remain under the patient during x-ray without interfering with imaging.

**Can HoverJack over-inflate/burst?** No — cannot be over-inflated to bursting when used with a HoverTech variable-speed air supply, under recommended conditions.

**HoverJack deflation:** Opening all red-capped valves at once causes rapid deflation which can destabilize the lift — open one at a time for slow, steady, stable deflation. Remains stable regardless of how many chambers are inflated, as long as inflated consecutively from the floor up.

**HoverMatt SPU breathability:** Breathable fabric lets air circulate under the patient, aiding evaporation and reducing heat/moisture on skin — reduces pressure-injury potential; can remain under the patient for the duration of the hospital stay.

**Infection control — Heat-Sealed vs Double-Coated:** Heat-sealed eliminates needle holes/thread as a route for microorganism entry (a risk with sewn products). Double-coated adds fluid/stain resistance via a silica polyurethane coating on the patient side. All reusable HoverMatts include an antimicrobial additive drawn through the nylon/polyurethane laminate if fluids contact the device.

## Preventative maintenance / storage
- Wipe down between patients (disinfectant wipes or 10:1 bleach solution); launder at 65°C max; air-dry preferred, never exceed 46°C if using a dryer; only air-dry double-coated models
- Do not reuse or clean SPUs
- Regular visual inspection of product, safety straps, and handles
- Storage: ensure valves aren't compressed when folded; keep inflation caps and safety straps/buckles contained in the storage basket; replace red deflation caps where applicable (damage often happens during storage, not use)

## Best-practice use technique
- "Stay Close" approach with the angled transfer technique for ergonomic positioning
- "Position of Safety": caregiver's body against the HoverMatt/HoverJack, hands on handles, for caregiver control

## Clinical context (why this range exists)
Falls are the leading cause of injury-related hospitalisation in Australia — 248,211 fall-injury hospitalisations in 2023–24 (~43% of all injury admissions), costing the health system over $5 billion in that year (AIHW data cited in JDHG training material). Musculoskeletal injury from manual patient handling/transfer/repositioning is a related, well-documented staff-safety issue this product range is designed to reduce.
