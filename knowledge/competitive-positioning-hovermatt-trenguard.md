# Competitive Positioning — HoverMatt + TrenGuard vs. Xodus Pink Pad Air-Assist

**Source type: internal sales positioning material, not a regulatory or manufacturer document.** Authored by Andrew Lau, shared as a Teams message on 2026-01-30. This is JDHG's own competitive comparison for rep use — treat any figures below sourced to "distributor material" as a marketing claim, not an independently verified clinical fact.
Curated: 2026-07-09.

## Comparison table: Xodus Pink Pad Air-Assist vs. HoverMatt + TrenGuard (D.A. Surgical)

| Category | Xodus Pink Pad Air-Assist | HoverMatt + TrenGuard |
|---|---|---|
| Intended use (per IFU) | Single-use air-assisted patient transfer and positioning surface | HoverMatt: air-assisted patient transfer surface. TrenGuard: reusable mechanical patient positioning system |
| Use model | Single-use / disposable | Hybrid system: HoverMatt (SPU or reusable options) + TrenGuard reusable capital accessory |
| Primary clinical role | Combined transfer + friction-based positioning in one product | Separation of functions: transfer (HoverMatt) and position locking (TrenGuard) are two distinct products |
| Positioning mechanism | Friction-based disposable surface | Mechanical support frame with trapezius bolsters (see TrenGuard IFU for the "Speed Bump" mechanism) |
| Trendelenburg rating (IFU) | Up to 35° | Up to 40° |
| Suitability for steep Trendelenburg | Moderate tilt only; performance depends on surface friction | Designed specifically for steep Trendelenburg and long procedures |
| Stability over long cases | Relies on surface friction — may be affected by moisture, case duration, or patient BMI | Mechanical restraint, independent of moisture, friction, or case length |
| High-BMI patient suitability | Limited by the friction-based design | Designed to accommodate high-BMI patients (see TrenGuard 600 pack, up to 250kg) |
| Reported clinical usage data | No published global "no-slide" case volume stated in its IFU | **~750,000 procedures globally and ~10,000 cases in Australia** — figure reported in distributor material, not an independently audited statistic |

## How to use this with a rep's question
This table compares the *combination* of HoverMatt (transfer) + TrenGuard (position-locking) against Xodus's single Pink Pad product, which does both in one disposable surface. The core positioning argument is the **separation of functions** — a dedicated mechanical restraint (TrenGuard) that isn't affected by moisture/friction/case-length, versus a single friction-based surface handling both jobs. The 40° vs 35° Trendelenburg rating and higher-BMI suitability are the other concrete differentiators.

If a rep asks for the source IFU-stated Trendelenburg rating for Xodus Pink Pad specifically, note that this comparison table is the only source currently in the knowledge base for that figure — it has not been independently verified against Xodus's own IFU.
