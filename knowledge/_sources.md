Note on the competitive-positioning file: this is sales/positioning material sourced from a Teams chat, not a manufacturer or regulatory document — a different content type from everything else in this knowledge base. The bot should present it as JDHG's internal positioning view, and treat the "~750,000 procedures globally" figure as a reported distributor claim, not a verified statistic. Teams chat was also found to contain internal competitive-strategy and product-design discussion unrelated to this comparison — that material was deliberately not brought into the knowledge base and should not be added without the same explicit request/review this comparison got.

Note on all HoverTech IFU files above: the public HoverTech International / HoverMatt.com manual pages returned HTTP 403 to direct web fetch (site blocks non-browser requests), so these were sourced from JD Healthcare's own SharePoint copy of the same manufacturer documents instead — same manufacturer text, not independently cross-checked against the live public PDF byte-for-byte. Newer revisions may exist on the manufacturer's site; re-check periodically. Full HoverTech range now covered: HoverMatt (incl. SPU/Link/Split-Leg/Half-Matt/OR/T-Burg variants), HoverJack, EMS Evacuation HoverJack, HoverSling (Split-Leg & Repositioning Sheet), Q2Roller, SitAssist Pro, and the HT-Air 2300 air supply. Two older manual revisions (HJManual Rev.G, T-Burg Rev.B, Q2Roller Rev.H, plus a duplicate HMManual copy) exist in the same SharePoint folder but were superseded by the newer revisions used here and intentionally not loaded.

Note on TrenGuard: JDHG's 2024 User Guide is treated as authoritative over the manufacturer's original 2015 IFU where the two disagree (reverse-Trendelenburg use, warranty period) — see the note inside trenguard-usage-and-ifu.md for specifics. A 600 Hybrid procedure pack ARTG number could not be confirmed from the documents reviewed; flag this to a rep asking specifically for it rather than guessing.

Deliberately excluded from this v1 knowledge base: the legacy "Info From Suppliers/HoverTech (Supplier) Info" folder (100+ files, 2011-2024, includes outdated competitor comparison matrices and superseded training decks), the large volume of historical TrenGuard hospital-specific evaluation forms/quotes scattered across individual rep folders (2019-2021), and any file that could not be confirmed as the current version. If asked about something only covered in that legacy material, the bot should say so rather than guess.
