## Intended purpose (per ARTG entries)
- **Support Frame (275210):** patient positioning accessory used in Gynecology, Urology, conventional and robotic-assisted laparoscopic procedures. Provides a system for positioning patients up to 250 kg in up to 40° steep Trendelenburg.
- **Lateral Stabilizing Pillows (290466):** soft, non-structural pillows controlling body mass shift during the transition from horizontal to extreme Trendelenburg; used with the Support Frame and Hybrid procedure pack.
- **450 Hybrid procedure pack (290464):** contains a single-use Cervical Neck Pad ("Speed Bump") and single-use Head Stabilizer. **This is the default pack for all patients weighing up to 200kg** — used with the Support Frame and Reusable Lateral Stabilizing Pillows.
