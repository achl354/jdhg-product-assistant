# EasiMove PRO — Reusable Air-Assisted Lateral Transfer Mattress (Manufacturer IFU)

**Content type: manufacturer Instructions for Use (IFU), current manufacturer document.** Source: DirectMed Group Pty Ltd, "EasiMovePRO™ Instructions for Use," Document MHDM-EM34PRO, **Rev A-1 (23/07/2026)** — this supersedes the earlier Rev A (16/06/2026); both were found in company SharePoint, Rev A-1 is the current revision (see Revision Information below).

## What it is

The EasiMovePRO™ is a reusable, RF-welded/heat-sealed air-assisted lateral transfer mattress for repeated multi-patient use. Intended to assist trained healthcare professionals with lateral patient transfer, repositioning, boosting, and turning by inflating (via a compatible air supply) to create a low-friction interface beneath the patient.

## Intended use and indications

- Lateral transfer between beds, stretchers, imaging tables, operating tables, and other compatible support surfaces
- Repositioning and boosting patients in bed; assisting with patient turning procedures
- Bariatric patient handling within the stated Safe Working Load
- Situations where manual patient handling risks caregiver musculoskeletal injury
- Patient transfer within ambulance/emergency transport environments where clinically appropriate
- Intended care environments: hospitals, operating theatres, ICUs, emergency departments, imaging departments, day surgery, ambulance/patient transport, long-term care facilities
- For use by trained healthcare professionals only

## Contraindications

Should not be used: for lifting or suspension of patients; without appropriate caregiver assistance; on patients with unstable cervical, thoracic, or lumbar fractures unless clinically assessed and approved; on inclined surfaces while inflated; if damaged, punctured, or unable to maintain inflation performance.

## Key warnings

- Always ensure transfer surfaces are stable and wheels are locked prior to transfer; never leave a patient unattended while inflated
- Minimum two caregivers during lateral transfer (more depending on patient size/condition/environment)
- Do not use Patient Safety Straps as Reinforced Transfer Handles
- Do not exceed the stated Safe Working Load
- **Always fully deflate the EasiMovePRO before tilting/angling the operating table** and ensure the patient is appropriately secured prior to Trendelenburg/reverse-Trendelenburg/other angled positioning — the device is **not** intended to function as a patient restraint
- Only use compatible air supply systems; do not use in the presence of flammable anaesthetic mixtures
- Printed Head Outline and Centre Alignment Line are visual positioning aids only, not a replacement for clinical judgement

## Product overview / key features

RF-welded construction; Printed Head Outline; Patient Safety Straps; Reinforced Transfer Handles; Centre Alignment Line; Product Identification Label; Inflation Port Connections. Designed for reusable, multi-patient use following appropriate cleaning/disinfection (unlike EasiMove SPU, which is single-patient-use only — see `easimove-spu-usage-and-ifu.md`).

## Operating room and imaging use

May be used within perioperative/imaging environments. Prior to any operating table articulation or angled positioning: turn off the air supply, fully deflate, disconnect the hose where required, ensure the patient is correctly positioned on the receiving surface, raise side rails per facility protocol. Where RFID-enabled linen management tags are present in the straps, position them clear of the imaging field if required.

## Cleaning, disinfection and laundering (Rev A-1, current)

- Between patient uses: wipe all external surfaces with hospital-grade neutral detergent or approved disinfectant, following the disinfectant manufacturer's contact-time instructions; ensure fully dry before reuse/storage
- If heavily soiled, may be machine laundered: recommended wash temperature 60°C, **maximum wash temperature 71°C**; a **1:10 bleach solution may be used**; low temperature tumble dry or air dry preferred, and **if tumble drying, do not exceed 46°C**
- Do not: autoclave, iron, dry clean, or use excessive heat drying cycles
- Remove from service if: tears/punctures identified, inflation performance compromised, handles/straps damaged, or seams separating/compromised

## Storage and transportation

Store in a clean, dry environment away from direct sunlight, excessive humidity, sharp objects, and excessive heat. Avoid prolonged compression or folding under heavy loads.
