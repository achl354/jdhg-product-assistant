# EasiMove — ARTG Certificates and Regulatory Information

**Content type: TGA Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG) certificates, current regulatory documents.** Sponsor for all entries below: DirectMed Pty Ltd / DirectMed Group Pty Ltd. Two ARTG entries were found referencing EasiMove-related devices — see the "NEEDS CONFIRMATION" note below before quoting either as definitively "the" EasiMove ARTG number to a customer.

## ARTG 528531 — Inflatable patient transfer sliding mattress

- **ARTG Identifier:** 528531
- **ARTG Start Date:** 3/07/2026
- **Product Category:** Medical Device Included Class 1
- **GMDN Code:** 65774 — **GMDN Term:** Inflatable patient transfer sliding mattress
- **Intended Purpose:** Reusable air-assisted patient transfer mattresses — the device is intended to assist with safe lateral transfers and repositioning by providing a low-friction, air-assisted sliding surface to reduce manual handling force requirements in clinical environments.
- **Manufacturer:** Dongguan Chengfeng Industrial Co Ltd, Gedi Industrial Zone Nancheng District, Dongguan City, Guangdong, China
- **Product-specific conditions:** None recorded.

This entry's source filename (provided directly by JDHG) identifies it as the **EasiMove PRO** ARTG certificate, and its GMDN term/code (65774, "Inflatable patient transfer sliding mattress") exactly matches the GMDN classification used for HoverTech's own ARTG entry for the equivalent device type (see `artg-certificate.md`) — the two most consistent, current-looking signals available. Very recently registered (3/07/2026), consistent with EasiMove's own June/July 2026 IFU launch timing.

## ARTG 343300 — Lift, &lt;specify&gt;

- **ARTG Identifier:** 343300
- **ARTG Start Date:** 11/09/2020
- **Product Category:** Medical Device Included Class 1
- **GMDN Code:** 12329 — **GMDN Term:** Lift, &lt;specify&gt;
- **Intended Purpose:** Air assisted lateral patient transfer mat
- **Manufacturer:** HongKong Ruikang Medical Technology Ltd, 0121/f Prosper Commercial Building, 9 Yin Chong Street, Kowloon, Hong Kong SAR of China

This entry was found filed in DirectMed's own "TGA Cert" folder alongside the EasiMove IFUs, so it's included here for completeness, but its GMDN term ("Lift, &lt;specify&gt;" — a generic/placeholder-sounding classification) doesn't cleanly match a lateral transfer mattress, its manufacturer differs from the one named on ARTG 528531, and it was registered in 2020 — several years before EasiMove's 2026 IFU launch.

## NEEDS CONFIRMATION — which ARTG number applies to which current EasiMove product

Two different ARTG identifiers (528531 and 343300) were found associated with EasiMove/DirectMed lateral transfer mattress material, with different manufacturers, different GMDN classifications, and start dates four-plus years apart. Separately, an internal JDHG product master spreadsheet excerpt was seen to flag its own internal reconciliation note to the effect of "EasiMove SPU shows [one ARTG number] vs [another] — both carried, flagged; reconcile" (the exact second number in that spreadsheet snippet did not match either 528531 or 343300 precisely, and the file was only available as a partial extract, so it could not be independently confirmed either).

**Do not present either 528531 or 343300 as definitively "the" ARTG number for EasiMove PRO, EasiMove SPU, or the EasiMove range generally.** If a rep needs to quote an ARTG number to a customer — especially for a tender, compliance document, or procurement request — tell them to confirm the correct, currently-applicable ARTG identifier (and which specific EasiMove product/product code it covers) with JDHG/Regulatory or DirectMed Group directly before doing so.

## Standard ARTG conditions (applies to both entries above)

Both entries are subject to standard ARTG conditions per the Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 (Part 4-5, Division 2) and Therapeutic Goods (Medical Devices) Regulations 2002 (Part 5, Division 5.2) — breaching conditions of inclusion may lead to suspension/cancellation of the entry and may attract criminal or civil penalties.
