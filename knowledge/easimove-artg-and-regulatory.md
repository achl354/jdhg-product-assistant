# EasiMove — ARTG Certificates and Regulatory Information

**Content type: TGA Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG) certificates, current regulatory documents.** Sponsor for all entries below: DirectMed Pty Ltd / DirectMed Group Pty Ltd.

## ARTG 528531 — Inflatable patient transfer sliding mattress (confirmed: EasiMove SPU)

- **ARTG Identifier:** 528531
- **ARTG Start Date:** 3/07/2026
- **Product Category:** Medical Device Included Class 1
- **GMDN Code:** 65774 — **GMDN Term:** Inflatable patient transfer sliding mattress
- **Intended Purpose:** Reusable air-assisted patient transfer mattresses — the device is intended to assist with safe lateral transfers and repositioning by providing a low-friction, air-assisted sliding surface to reduce manual handling force requirements in clinical environments.
- **Manufacturer:** Dongguan Chengfeng Industrial Co Ltd, Gedi Industrial Zone Nancheng District, Dongguan City, Guangdong, China
- **Product-specific conditions:** None recorded.

**Confirmed by JDHG (2026-07-29): 528531 is the ARTG number for EasiMove SPU.** This entry's source filename was originally saved by JDHG as "EasiMove PRO ARTG," so its applicability to EasiMove PRO specifically hasn't been separately re-confirmed since this SPU clarification — if a rep needs an ARTG number specifically for EasiMove PRO (as distinct from SPU), confirm with JDHG/Regulatory before assuming the same number applies, even though both products share the same GMDN device classification.

## ARTG 343300 — Lift, &lt;specify&gt;

- **ARTG Identifier:** 343300
- **ARTG Start Date:** 11/09/2020
- **Product Category:** Medical Device Included Class 1
- **GMDN Code:** 12329 — **GMDN Term:** Lift, &lt;specify&gt;
- **Intended Purpose:** Air assisted lateral patient transfer mat
- **Manufacturer:** HongKong Ruikang Medical Technology Ltd, 0121/f Prosper Commercial Building, 9 Yin Chong Street, Kowloon, Hong Kong SAR of China
- **Product-specific conditions:** None recorded.

This entry was found filed in DirectMed's own "TGA Cert" folder alongside the EasiMove IFUs. Its GMDN term ("Lift, &lt;specify&gt;" — a generic/placeholder-sounding classification) doesn't cleanly match a lateral transfer mattress, its manufacturer differs from the one named on ARTG 528531, and it was registered in 2020, several years before EasiMove's 2026 IFU launch. **Not confirmed to apply to any current EasiMove product** — do not present 343300 as an EasiMove ARTG number without confirming with JDHG/Regulatory first.
