# EasiAir — Variable Speed Air Supply (Manufacturer IFU)

**Content type: manufacturer Instructions for Use (IFU), current manufacturer document.** Source: DirectMed Group Pty Ltd, "EasiAir™ Instructions for Use," Document MHDM-EA-IFU-001, Rev A (05/06/2026).

## What it is

The EasiAir™ Variable Speed Air Supply is a mains-powered, portable air supply providing controlled airflow to compatible DirectMed Group-approved air-assisted patient handling devices. It does not directly support patient weight and is not intended for direct patient contact — always refer to the connected device's own IFU for Safe Working Load and patient handling instructions.

**Compatible DirectMed Group devices (per this IFU):** EasiMove™ SPU, EasiMove™ PRO, EasiLift™ Air Patient Lift, EasiTurn™. (Also cross-referenced as compatible from the EasiMove PRO/SPU IFUs with HoverTech® HT-Air® 2300 Air Supply as an additional compatible air source — that compatibility statement is on the EasiMove side, not restated here.) If compatibility with a particular device is uncertain, contact DirectMed Group Pty Ltd before use.

## Indications for use

Wherever controlled airflow is required to operate compatible DirectMed Group-approved air-assisted patient handling systems: lateral patient transfer, patient repositioning, patient turning, post-fall recovery procedures, air-assisted patient lifting procedures, bariatric patient handling (within the connected device's stated SWL), and reduction of manual handling forces.

Intended operators: healthcare professionals trained in patient handling procedures, healthcare workers trained in operating compatible air-assisted devices, and personnel trained in applicable facility policies. **Not intended to be operated by patients.** Not a life-support device; not for respiratory support, ventilation, or airway management.

## Contraindications

Should not be used: with devices not approved by DirectMed Group Pty Ltd; if the device, hose, connector, power cord, or plug is damaged; if the housing is cracked/broken/compromised; if abnormal noise, vibration, smell, overheating, or airflow is observed; if the device has been dropped and damage is suspected; if fluid ingress is suspected; in the presence of flammable anaesthetic mixtures; in wet environments; if the air inlet/filter/hose/outlet is blocked; for any purpose other than supplying air to compatible DirectMed Group-approved devices.

## Key warnings

- Use only with DirectMed Group-approved devices; do not modify the device (no modification permitted)
- The EasiAir does **not** determine patient weight limits — always refer to the connected device's IFU for Safe Working Load
- Do not block the air inlet, filter assembly, or air outlet; do not operate with a blocked/kinked/damaged hose; do not use in ambient temperatures above 40°C
- Incorporates thermal overload protection — if it stops unexpectedly, turn off power, allow to cool, inspect for airflow restrictions before returning to service
- **Electrical safety:** mains-powered device — verify suitable power supply, only connect to a properly installed outlet, never operate with wet hands, never immerse in water, disconnect by gripping the plug (not the cord), disconnect from mains before cleaning/inspection/maintenance/transport/storage
- If electrical damage, smoke, unusual odour, overheating, or abnormal operation occurs: stop use, disconnect from mains when safe, remove from service, and contact DirectMed Group Pty Ltd

## Product overview

Portable variable-speed air supply with a high-performance low-noise motor and adjustable airflow control. Key components: Carry Handle, Power Switch, Variable Speed Control, Fuse Holder, Power Indicator Light, Flexible Air Hose, Air Outlet Connection, Air Inlet Filter.

## Cleaning and disinfection

Clean and disinfect between patient uses and whenever visibly soiled: turn off and disconnect from mains, disconnect the hose, wipe external surfaces and hose exterior/connector with a neutral detergent or approved hospital-grade disinfectant, allow the recommended contact time, allow to dry completely before reuse.

**Do not:** immerse in water, autoclave, steam clean, machine wash, use high-pressure water jets, use abrasive cleaners, or use solvents that may damage plastics/labels. If fluid ingress is suspected, remove from service immediately and contact DirectMed Group Pty Ltd.

## Troubleshooting (selected)

| Problem | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| Device will not start | No mains power / fuse failure | Check outlet and plug connection / replace fuse |
| Reduced airflow | Blocked filter / kinked hose / loose connection | Clean filter / inspect hose / reconnect hose |
| Device stops unexpectedly | Thermal protection activated | Allow cooling, inspect airflow path |
| Burning smell | Electrical fault | Disconnect and remove from service |

Fuse replacement should only be performed by appropriately trained personnel; if a replacement fuse also fails, remove from service and contact DirectMed Group Pty Ltd.

## Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC)

Tested for EMC per applicable medical electrical equipment standards. Portable/mobile RF communications equipment may affect performance — avoid operating immediately adjacent to high-power RF equipment; if abnormal operation occurs, stop use, verify power supply integrity, relocate nearby RF equipment if possible, and remove from service if abnormal operation persists.

## Storage, disposal, and service

Store in a clean, dry environment protected from moisture, dust, direct sunlight, excessive heat, chemical exposure, and physical damage; perform a full inspection before returning to service after prolonged storage. Dispose per local regulations and e-waste requirements. Only DirectMed Group Pty Ltd or an authorised service provider should perform repairs beyond what's described in this IFU.
