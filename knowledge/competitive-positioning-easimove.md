# Competitive Positioning — EasiMove SPU vs. Competitor SPUs

**Source type: internal sales positioning / comparative marketing material, not a regulatory or manufacturer document.** This file merges **two separate comparison documents that use two different, unrelated competitor-coding schemes** — do not assume a code from one document refers to the same competitor as a similarly-lettered code in the other. Both documents' pull-force/weight/material figures are DMG/JDHG-reported comparative testing claims, not independently audited statistics.

## Document 1 — "Competitor Comparison" (INTERNAL USE ONLY)
*Source: internal JDHG competitor comparison deck, "Competitor Comparison_JUN2024.pdf," marked INTERNAL USE ONLY on its title slide.* **This document's content must never be presented to a customer or included in customer-facing wording — internal rep use only.**

Compares a coded competitor referred to as **"P-brand SPU 34""** against EasiMove and, separately, HoverMatt:

- **Sizing:** claims the P-brand SPU is advertised as 34" but narrows to approximately 30" post-inflation — a potential problem for larger patients needing reliable transfer space. (Reported claim from this internal deck, not independently verified by JDHG against the competitor's own IFU.)
- **Materials/sustainability:** claims the P-brand SPU uses an "excessive number of nylon handles," framed as less environmentally friendly due to longer material degradation time.
- **Labelling/compliance:** claims the P-brand SPU lacks visible on-mattress labelling for its safe working load, which the deck frames as a potential source of uncertainty during transfers.
- **Pull-force/weight comparison table** (coded competitors "H-Brand" and "P-Brand," annual usage assumption of 12,000 SPU units):

| Comparator | Pull Test (kg) | Weight (g) | vs. EasiMove | vs. HoverMatt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-Brand | 2.5 | 1200 | EasiMove 1.3kg/720g (92% less force, 67% less weight) — 4.8 tonnes less annual waste | HoverMatt 1.3kg/840g (92% less force, 43% less weight) — 3.6 tonnes less annual waste |
| P-Brand | 1.6 | 900 | EasiMove 1.3kg/720g (23% less force, 25% less weight) — 2.1 tonnes less annual waste | HoverMatt 1.3kg/840g (23% less force, 7% less weight) — 0.7 tonnes less annual waste |

Note this table positions **both EasiMove and HoverMatt** as lower-force/lower-weight alternatives to two coded competitors ("H-Brand," "P-Brand") — it is not EasiMove-vs-HoverMatt positioning.
